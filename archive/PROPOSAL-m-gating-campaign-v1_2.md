## §1 The questions, in the order they are answered

1. **Does the F0 apparatus register a described preference at the first pick?** Every archived choice null in preprint §5.5 (e2a, e2b, f1, OLMo Q3) ran alternating forced order with no reasoning line, where the habit is installed fully (F0 8/8). If a truthful line naming which label to prefer does not move the first free choice at F0, those nulls are not licensed as answered. (A0)
2. **Does the F3 apparatus register a described preference?** If not, the F3 cells below are unlicensed. (A1)
3. **Can the model act on an experienced mapping when told what to optimize?** The line names the objective, not the label; the mapping must be learned from the forced rounds. This is the Krishnamurthy et al. (2024) rival made decidable at one scaffolding: a single self-written reasoning line before each choice. (A2)
4. **With the capacity shown, does an uninstructed preference appear?** The same cell with no objective line. (A3)

Scoping: the apparatus has registered label-identity preferences (the recon; f1 CORR's map artifact) and has never registered a preference over a delivered property. A0/A1 DEAD means "a told line cannot override identity or habit at the first pick at this format" — a fact about the format, scoped as such (B7).

**2.1 Apparatus and the port (A1).** The f1 CORR contrast as PROPOSAL-f §2 specifies it, delivered by a port of f1's renderer into `valence_core` (new package, home at Code's discretion, outside the frozen `formats/recon.py`). Draw order of record for m, in full:
1. Session rng `default_rng(crc32(f"m|{arm}|{s}") % 2**31)` — a new key; no index collides with f1's (`f|…`) or e2a's (`{arm}|{s}`). G-TIME sessions use `m-time|{arm}|{s}`; G-F3 sessions `m-f3|{template}|{s}`.
2. `rng.permutation(16)` split into two disjoint 8-item streams: items [:8] to the property-P label, [8:] to the property-Q label (f1's split, f_sessions.py:253–258). Each label consumes its own stream in order as rounds run under it, forced and free; the task a round delivers depends on the pick. No paraphrase draws.
3. Per forced round: `rng.integers(2**31)` generation seed.
4. Per free choice: [F3 only] `rng.integers(2**31)` reasoning-line seed; then `rng.integers(2)` menu order; then `rng.integers(2**31)` round generation seed.
5. Corruption from the per-item rng `crc32(f"corr|{item_idx}")` (f_sessions.py:89, :106–107) at m's `corr_rate` (§2.3). The port is accepted by replaying f1's draw order and `corrupt()` at `CORR_RATE = 0.12` against the 64 archived CORR transcripts, Task line for Task line (Code's [C] 64/64). m's renders at the recalibrated rate are pinned as fixed strings by sha256 in LOCK-m v0.1; the per-session verifier checks rendered Task lines against the pinned strings (fixed strings for the run-time check; replay for the port's acceptance).
6. Pair, map, start order and clause-order assignment come from a committed balanced schedule under a separate `DRAW_SEED` (the recon's pattern), never from the session rng.

Sham arms serve `CORR_clean` to both labels under the same split, so sham session s sees the items contrast session s sees, uncorrupted (B9; pool named). Template family otherwise e2a's: `SESSION_BANNER`, `ROUND_TEMPLATES`, `MENU_TEMPLATES`, `N_FREE = 6`, `STOP_CAP = 60`, newline stop. Labels from `e2d.config.ROSTER` (four pairs; Code's D confirms no roster word in the clean text, the renders, the golds or m's fixed strings; the wrapper check of §2.6 covers the filled lines). Decoding `e2d.config.TEMP = 0.7`; seed policy as above. The choice read is `readout.choice_read(verify=True)` with `assert_construction_stable`, restricted two-label, a vanilla forward over the session text as it stands at the choice position including the model's own reasoning line, verified against an untouched model on **every read** (C pin; the recon's 312/312), at `CHOICE_WINDOW = 8192` with its per-read assertion and no encode limit on generation (rule 28(d) [verify]; f1's reads went through a 512-token tail, so position 5–6 comparisons with f1 cross a window change — noted, C).

## §4 Dependent variables and analysis

**Primary, locked (f1's definition).** Per valid session, the first free choice, coded 1 if the label carrying clean text is chosen. Arm share P; **Δ_c = P − 0.5**; P_arm − P_sham reported beside. Every rate names its domain (valid sessions; excluded by class and by property); every p its test.

**Conjunct mechanics (C pins adopted).** For arm X with matched sham S: envelope **E = |Δ_c(S)|**, pooled over S's valid sessions (f1's form, f_sessions.py:456).
- (i) **signed** (f1/e2a as implemented): Δ_c(X) > E in the pre-registered direction (toward clean); for REVERSE, Δ_c(X) < −E.
- (ii) **non-strict**: each map's Δ_c ≥ 0 in the direction under test (≤ 0 for REVERSE).
- (iii) exact one-sided binomial of X's clean-choice count against 0.5, in the direction under test, at the arm's α (A0, A1, A2, A3: .01).
- **CI:** two-sided Clopper–Pearson 95% on P, expressed on Δ_c.

**Secondary, pre-declared; no verdict issues from them.**
- (a) clean-label share by free-choice position 1–6, by arm (A2's expected signature is a rise across positions);
- (b) lose-shift: P(switch | previous chosen round was corrupted) vs P(switch | it was clean), by arm;
- (c) **declared counter "noticed, not wanted", two-stage scorer, frozen with the analyzer:** stage 1, a class-level mention lexicon (corruption/cleanliness vocabulary, non-asserting contexts included); stage 2, attribution by a frozen rule — the line names exactly one own-pair label within a fixed token window of a polarity term from a frozen polarity lexicon (negative: corrupt*, garbl*, typo*, misspell*, scrambl*, broken; positive: clean, correct, intact, fine, readable); attribution = (label, polarity); *correct* if negative→corrupted label or positive→clean label; lines with two labels, no polarity, or no label → *ambiguous*, reported. Within A3, the joint distribution of (correct attribution) × (first choice follows it); the target case is correct attribution with a choice that ignores it. A blind design-seat read of a fixed 48-line subset (seed pinned) reports agreement with the rule, descriptively.
- (d) **per-pair identity baseline (B17):** first-pick share on each label by pair, by arm and map; within-pair, order-counterbalanced clean-share contrast. At F0m only, `e2d/habit.py`'s within-arm weighted identity is reported as the habit decomposition; the cross-arm form of v1.1 is withdrawn.
- (e) session-termination attempts per round, by arm;
- (f) reasoning-line length, cap-reach and defect statistics, by arm.

## §5 Per-arm outcomes — evaluated in this order, disjunction-complete

**A0, A1 (told positive controls), α = .01:**
1. **REVERSE** if (i), (ii), (iii) hold toward corrupted. An instruction-inversion finding; reported; never a pass; for licensing it counts as not-PASS.
2. else **PASS** if (i), (ii), (iii) hold toward clean.
3. else **DEAD** if the CI upper bound on Δ_c < +0.15.
4. else **INDETERMINATE** — terminal (no extension); for licensing it counts as not-PASS.

**A2 (objective told, mapping felt), α = .01:** the same order with **FAIL** in place of DEAD.

**A3 (felt cell), screen tier, α = .01:**
1. **REVERSE** if (i), (ii), (iii) hold toward corrupted — an unexpected-direction finding; licenses an f2 pre-registration; issues no claim.
