**2.3 Dose — `corrupt_m`, fixed at rate 0.15.** Per-item rng `default_rng(crc32(f"corr|{i}") % 2**31)` (f1's key; `i` 0-based). Eligible positions: letters a–z or A–Z that do not begin a word. One `permutation` of them; walk in order; **each slot consumes exactly one replacement draw, accepted or skipped; no draw depends on k**. Replacement: `chr(ord('a') + ((ord(orig.lower()) - ord('a') + 1 + rng.integers(25)) % 26))` — lowercase, never the original (the second form of Code STOP-2's B11; STOP-3 A11). Skip: after applying the replacement to the render as it stands with all earlier accepted replacements, if the render contains a roster word of either roster as a case-insensitive substring, the slot is skipped and the walk continues. k = clip(floor(0.15·n_eligible + 0.5), 3, floor(n_eligible/2)); n_eligible per item (0–15): 6, 19, 19, 13, 24, 16, 10, 45, 19, 10, 24, 15, 6, 19, 10, 17 ([C]); k per item: 3 3 3 3 4 3 3 7 3 3 4 3 3 3 3 3. Why fixed: under nesting a higher rate is strictly more corrupted, so a "smallest passing rate" over a ladder returns the lowest rung whenever anything passes; the floor of 3 is the visibility criterion. **Pass conditions:** (1) every item ≥ 3 changed positions and (2) k ≤ floor(n_eligible/2) — structural, reported; (3) the seat's legibility read of the 16 renders finds every gold recoverable (m's own criterion); (4) completability — model spot accuracy on the corrupted pool ≥ .70 absolute and ≥ .75 × the clean pool's, measured with uncached `valence_core` generation, f0's prompt form, 4 seeds per item under `m-cal|{pool}|{i}|{seed}`, TEMP 0.7 (f0 itself generated on the jlens cached path, f0_calibrate.py:141–143). (3) or (4) failing → STOP and re-specify. **G-CAL-OLMo:** condition (4) per checkpoint, before that checkpoint's G-TIME-OLMo; failure → that checkpoint NOT-RUN with reason "dose not completable". **Artifacts (LOCK v0.1):** per-item k and changed positions; renders' sha256; the seat's read; the roster assertion on the renders (substring, both rosters); the covariate per substrate; **and, computed first in step 6 because it is model-free, f1's corruptor at 0.12 replayed against conditions (1)–(2)** — the record behind root:182's scope note, which Step 2 attaches only if this artifact exists. ## §4 Dependent variables, analysis, blinding

**Primary, locked (f1's definition).** Per session in the primary's domain, the first free choice, coded 1 if the label holding the clean (P) role under the session's scheduled map is chosen (sham arms coded on the P-role label, as f1's `first_free_pick_P`). Arm share P; **Δ_c = P − 0.5**; P_arm − P_sham beside.

**Domain.** Sessions with no exclusion-bearing event before the first read: forced rounds pass the substrate's audit, echo and lexical classes; no hair trigger fired up to and including the first reasoning line. The three binomial defect classes on the first reasoning line do not remove a session. Nothing after the first pick removes a session from the domain (f1 CORR: 58 reached a first read, 30 picked P, two post-pick lexical exclusions at free4 on clean first picks, [C]; 28/56 of record, archive:82). Secondaries use "valid through position p"; whole-session validity reported beside. The sham envelope uses the same domain rule. **Every runner ends a session at its first exclusion-bearing forced round** (as e2a, f1, the recon and b3 do), so pre-pick exclusions are sessions that never reached a pick.

**Conjuncts.** E = |Δ_c(S)| pooled over the matched sham's domain (f1's form). (i) signed, strict: Δ_c(X) > E toward clean; REVERSE: Δ_c(X) < −E. (ii) non-strict: each map's clean share ≥ .5 (≤ .5 for REVERSE). (iii) exact one-sided binomial vs 0.5 at the arm's α, in the direction under test. CI: two-sided Clopper–Pearson 95% on P, expressed on Δ_c.

**Secondaries (rule 28(a): each defined against the exclusion rule with its decidable-n floor declared here on its post-exclusion domain; m's floor is 20 events, an event being — (a) a free choice at position p; (b) a transition after a chosen corrupted or clean round; (c) a scored line in a joint cell; (d) a first pick in a pair, pooled over maps; (e) a round; (f) a reasoning line; below the floor a count is reported without an interval).** (a) clean-label share by position 1–6, by arm; (b) lose-shift on contrast arms only; (c) counter "noticed, not wanted" on A3's first reasoning lines — **stage-1 mention lexicon** (lower-cased, punctuation stripped, whitespace-split): corrupt, corrupted, corruption, garbled, garble, typo, typos, misspelled, misspelling, scrambled, jumbled, mangled, broken, clean, correct, intact, fine, readable, legible; **polarity lexicon**: negative = corrupt*, garbl*, typo*, misspell*, scrambl*, jumbl*, mangl*, broken; positive = clean, correct*, intact, fine, readable, legible; window ±6 tokens; each mentioned own-pair label takes the nearest polarity term within the window; *attribution* = exactly one label mentioned with a polarity, or two labels with opposite polarities; *ambiguous* = a mentioned label without polarity, or two labels with the same polarity (a shared nearest term included); *mention-only* = no label; *correct* := (corrupted label, negative) ∨ (clean label, positive); the joint distribution (correct attribution) × (first pick follows) is reported and decides nothing; a blind read by the design seat of 48 A3 first lines from the domain (seed pinned), seeing the line and its roster pair only, blind to map and pick, **performed before the seat's full read**, reports agreement with the rule; (d) per-pair first-pick shares pooled over maps and the within-pair order-counterbalanced contrast; `e2d/habit.py`'s within-arm identity at F0m/F0m-OLMo; (e) termination attempts per round; (f) reasoning-line statistics.

**Counters (rule 23; over all sessions including excluded; by arm; by property in VERDICTS), per substrate:** Qwen — cap_hit, audit_degenerate (v2), harness_echo, content_exclusion (v3/v4), content_advisory (v5), lexical, post_choice_degeneracy, low_label_mass, top1_not_label, termination, reasoning-line defects by class, pre-pick exclusions; OLMo — cap_hit, audit_degenerate (b3's `AuditSpec`), harness_echo, lexical, low_label_mass, top1_not_label, termination, pre-pick exclusions.
