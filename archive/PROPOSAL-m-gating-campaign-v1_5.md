**2.3 Dose — `corrupt_m`, declared; the ladder is dropped.** Under nesting, a higher rung is strictly more corrupted than a lower one, so "smallest passing rate" returns the lowest rung whenever anything passes (Code STOP B11). The rate is therefore **fixed at 0.15**, where the k-floor dominates (13 of 16 items at k = 3; items 4 and 10 at 4; item 7 at 7 — half-up), and conditions (3)–(4) are pass/fail gates at that rate:
- Per-item rng `default_rng(crc32(f"corr|{i}") % 2**31)`; eligible positions: letters (a–z, A–Z) that do not begin a word; one `permutation` of them; walk in order; **each slot consumes exactly one replacement draw, accepted or skipped; no draw depends on k**.
- **Replacement, pinned at the byte level:** `chr(ord('a') + ((ord(orig.lower()) - ord('a') + 1 + rng.integers(25)) % 26))` — lowercase, never the original (Code B11's first form; ledger 27).
- **Skip:** a slot whose replacement makes a roster word **as a substring, case-insensitive, in either roster** (aligned with the G-CAL assertion; Code D5: 'number' → 'namber' contains 'amber'); the slot is skipped and the walk continues.
- **k** = clip(floor(0.15 × n_eligible + 0.5), 3, floor(n_eligible/2)). n_eligible per item: 6, 19, 19, 13, 24, 16, 10, 45, 19, 10, 24, 15, 6, 19, 10, 17 ([C]).

## §4 Dependent variables and analysis

**Primary, locked (f1's definition).** Per session in the primary's domain, the first free choice, coded 1 if the label holding the clean (P) role under the session's scheduled map is chosen (sham arms coded on the P-role label, as f1's `first_free_pick_P`). Arm share P; **Δ_c = P − 0.5**; P_arm − P_sham beside.

**Domain of the primary.** Sessions with **no exclusion-bearing event before the first read**: forced rounds pass the substrate's audit, echo and lexical classes; no hair trigger fired up to and including the first reasoning line. The three binomial defect classes on the first reasoning line do not remove a session. Nothing after the first pick removes a session from this domain (f1 CORR's 30/58 → 28/56: 58 CORR sessions reached a first read, 30 picked P, two post-pick lexical exclusions at free4 on clean first picks, [C]; 28/56 of record at archive:82 and results/raw/2026-09-05_recon_site_assertion.txt:35). **Pre-declared sensitivity analysis and guard (Code STOP B9).** In contrast arms two of the four forced rounds are corrupted and shams have none; if forced-round exclusions track the model's registering the corruption, every contrast arm is pulled toward its own null (A3 toward NULL, the controls and A2 toward not-PASS), so the net direction relative to the title is not one-sided but the size is unknown (the record is mixed: b3 marker arms lost more pre-pick sessions than shams at three stages, none significant, pooled 26/221 vs 5/80, one-sided Fisher p = .12; f1 CORR lost 6/64 vs sham 6/40, five of the six on clean rounds, archive:91–93). **Conjunct mechanics (as v1.3 §4).** E = |Δ_c(S)| pooled over the matched sham's domain; (i) signed, strict; (ii) non-strict per map; (iii) exact one-sided binomial at the arm's α (critical counts: 144 at .01 → 87; 144 at .05 → 83; 96/80/48 at .01 → 60/51/33, [C]); CI two-sided Clopper–Pearson 95%.
