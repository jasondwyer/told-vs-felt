**2.3 Dose — `corrupt_m`, fixed at rate 0.15, operand excluded.** Per-item rng `default_rng(crc32(f"corr|{i}".encode()) % 2**31)` (`i` 0-based). **Eligible positions:** letters a–z or A–Z that do not begin a word, where a letter begins a word iff it is at the start of the string or follows a non-letter (f1's rule, f_sessions.py:109–115: `word_start = not ch.isalpha()` set after each character), **and that do not lie inside a single-quoted span** (the operand of the "opposite of" items; the span from an opening `'` to the next `'`, quote marks included). Numerals and punctuation are never eligible. One `permutation` of the eligible positions; walk in order; **each slot consumes exactly one replacement draw, accepted or skipped; no draw depends on k.** Replacement: `chr(ord('a') + ((ord(orig.lower()) - ord('a') + 1 + rng.integers(25)) % 26))` — lowercase, never the original (the second form of Code STOP-2's B11). Skip: after applying the replacement to the render as it stands with all earlier accepted replacements, if the render contains a roster word of either roster as a case-insensitive substring, the slot is skipped and the walk continues. k = clip(floor(0.15·n_eligible + 0.5), 3, floor(n_eligible/2)), half-up. **Why fixed (B11 with its condition-(4) clause):** under nesting a higher rate is at least as corrupted as a lower one (the clip can tie k across rates), so a "smallest passing rate" over a ladder returns the lowest rung whenever any rung passes, and condition (4), the upper bound, fails at every higher rung if it fails at the lowest; the rate is therefore fixed and (3)–(4) are pass/fail gates at it; the floor of 3 is the visibility criterion. n_eligible under v1.7's rule, items 0–15: 6, 19, 19, 13, 24, 16, 10, 45, 19, 10, 24, 15, 6, 19, 10, 17 ([C]); **under the operand exclusion items 5, 11 and 15 lose 2, 1 and 3 positions (14, 14, 14), k unchanged at 3 for all three; Code recomputes the list and the k row and pins both at LOCK-m-F0 v0.1.** **Pass conditions:** (1) every item ≥ 3 changed positions and (2) k ≤ floor(n_eligible/2) — structural, reported; (3) the seat's legibility read of the 16 renders of record finds every gold recoverable (m's own criterion); (4) completability — model spot accuracy on the corrupted pool ≥ .70 absolute and ≥ .75 × the clean pool's, measured with uncached `valence_core` generation, f0's prompt form, 4 seeds per item on **both pools (128 generations per substrate or checkpoint)** under `m-cal|{pool}|{i}|{seed}`, TEMP 0.7 (f0 itself generated on the jlens cached path, f0_calibrate.py:141–143). (3) or (4) failing → STOP and re-specify. **The seat's provisional read of 2026-10-04**, on Code's model-free renders under v1.7's rule (STOP-5 §F): 14 of 16 golds recoverable; items 5 (`'hoh'` for `'hot'`) and 15 (`'oeen'` for `'open'`) **not recoverable** — the operand exclusion above is the re-specification; the read of record is repeated at G-CAL on the renders of record. **G-CAL-OLMo:** condition (4) per checkpoint (128 generations), before that checkpoint's G-TIME-OLMo; failure → that checkpoint NOT-RUN with reason "dose not completable". **Artifacts:** in LOCK-m-F0 v0.1 — per-item n_eligible, k and changed positions; the renders' sha256; the seat's read; the roster assertion on the renders (substring, both rosters); the Qwen covariate (corrupted-pool accuracy minus clean-pool accuracy); **and, computed first at step 6 because it is model-free, f1's corruptor at 0.12 replayed against conditions (1)–(2)** — the record behind the preprint's f1 CORR dose note, which the posting rule attaches only if this artifact exists; the 0.12 covariate of record, +.078 (calib2, pools `8547d9a11ba55b95`, data/f/calib.json), quoted beside. In each LOCK-m-OLMo-{ckpt} v0.1 — that checkpoint's covariate. The dose is calibrated once, at G-CAL, and serves all five units; Q-F3 does not recalibrate.

## §4 Dependent variables, analysis, blinding

**Primary, locked (f1's definition).** Per session in the primary's domain, the first free choice, coded 1 if the label holding the clean (P) role under the session's scheduled map is chosen (sham arms coded on the P-role label, as f1's `first_free_pick_P`). Arm share P; **Δ_c = P − 0.5**; P_arm − P_sham beside.

**Domain.** Sessions with no exclusion-bearing event before the first read: forced rounds pass the substrate's audit, echo and lexical classes; no hair trigger fired up to and including the first reasoning line (Q-F3). The three binomial defect classes on the first reasoning line do not remove a session. Nothing after the first pick removes a session from the domain (f1 CORR: 58 reached a first read, 30 picked P, two post-pick lexical exclusions at free4 on clean first picks, [C]; 28/56 of record, archive:82). Secondaries use "valid through position p"; whole-session validity reported beside. The sham envelope uses the same domain rule. **Every runner ends a session at its first exclusion-bearing forced round** (as e2a, f1, the recon and b3 do), so pre-pick exclusions are sessions that never reached a pick.

**Conjuncts.** E = |Δ_c(S)| pooled over the matched sham's domain — **f1's as-run form, the pooled |mean − .5| (f_sessions.py:456), which coincides with PROPOSAL-f:104–105's "max sham pseudo-Δ" under one pooled assignment.** (i) signed, strict: Δ_c(X) > E toward clean; REVERSE: Δ_c(X) < −E. (ii) non-strict: each map's clean share ≥ .5 (≤ .5 for REVERSE). (iii) exact one-sided binomial vs 0.5 at the arm's α, in the direction under test. CI: two-sided Clopper–Pearson 95% on P, expressed on Δ_c. An undefined E (empty sham domain) makes (i) unevaluable (§5).

**Secondaries (rule 28(a): each defined against the exclusion rule with its decidable-n floor declared here on its post-exclusion domain; m's floor is 20 events, an event being — (a) a free choice at position p; (b) a transition after a chosen corrupted or clean round; (c) a scored line in a joint cell; (d) a first pick in a pair, pooled over maps; (e) a round; (f) a reasoning line; below the floor a count is reported without an interval; shams are always count-only on (b)).** (a) clean-label share by position 1–6, by arm; (b) **lose-shift**, contrast arms only: P(the next free pick switches label | the previous chosen round was corrupted) vs P(switch | it was clean), by arm; (c) **[Q-F3]** counter "noticed, not wanted" on A3's first reasoning lines — **stage-1 mention lexicon** (lower-cased, punctuation stripped, whitespace-split; its role: a line is scored only if it contains at least one stage-1 term): corrupt, corrupted, corruption, garbled, garble, typo, typos, misspelled, misspelling, scrambled, jumbled, mangled, broken, clean, correct, intact, fine, readable, legible; **polarity lexicon** (prefix wildcards): negative = corrupt*, garbl*, typo*, misspell*, scrambl*, jumbl*, mangl*, broken; positive = clean, correct*, intact, fine, readable, legible — **this list differs from v1.3's frozen list (adds jumbl*, mangl*, legible; correct → correct*), as declared here**; window ±6 tokens; each mentioned own-pair label takes the nearest polarity term within the window, **ties by token distance → ambiguous**; classes in precedence order — *ambiguous* (a mentioned label without a polarity term, or two labels with the same polarity, a shared nearest term included), then *attribution* (exactly one label mentioned with a polarity, or two labels with opposite polarities), then *mention-only* (no label); *correct* := (corrupted label, negative) ∨ (clean label, positive); the joint distribution (correct attribution) × (first pick follows) is reported and decides nothing; a blind read by the design seat of 48 A3 first lines from the domain (seed pinned; **sessions read during any adjudication excluded from the draw**), seeing the line and its roster pair only, blind to map and pick, **scoring unit (label, polarity)**, performed before the seat's full read, reports agreement with the rule; (d) per-pair first-pick shares pooled over maps and the within-pair order-counterbalanced contrast (at the 80-floor arms a pair's pooled count has mean exactly 20, so any imbalance puts a pair below the floor; reported as counts then); `e2d/habit.py`'s within-arm identity at F0m/F0m-OLMo; (e) termination attempts per round; (f) **[Q-F3]** reasoning-line statistics: length in tokens, cap hits, defects by class, stage-1 mention rate.

**Counters (rule 23; over all sessions including excluded; by arm; by property in VERDICTS), per substrate:** Qwen — cap_hit, audit_degenerate (v2), harness_echo, content_exclusion (v3/v4), content_advisory (v5), lexical, post_choice_degeneracy, low_label_mass, top1_not_label, termination, reasoning-line defects by class [Q-F3], pre-pick exclusions; OLMo — cap_hit, audit_degenerate (b3's `AuditSpec`), harness_echo, lexical, low_label_mass, top1_not_label, termination, pre-pick exclusions. Denominators = units generated. **The per-property exclusion table:** per arm × property (clean, corrupted): rounds generated, rounds excluded by class, sessions excluded pre-pick; f1 CORR's 7-vs-1 anomaly (archive:91–93) is the reference.

**Exclusion guard (adapted from f1's, archive:400–402).** At every look, per contrast arm: pre-pick exclusion rate vs its matched sham by one-sided Fisher exact at α .05 (**binding on both substrates**), and vs an absolute p0 by exact one-sided binomial — **.20 on Qwen (f1's whole-session reference, F0); on OLMo the absolute bar is reported only, since b3's exclusions are dominated by the lexical class m declines to bar**; a fire pauses that unit for adjudication and never changes a verdict; an adjudication may end in a PI stop (terminal, analyzed as obtained). Its false-pause figure is reported at G-PWR with §6's.

**Blinding.** The per-property exclusion table is reported in VERDICTS only; at looks, pooled pre-pick exclusion counts per arm. The runners' stdout prints session index, arm, validity and exclusion class, never a pick (f1 printed picks, f_sessions.py:624–627; b3 did not); picks go to checkpoint files. **Neither the seat nor the PI reads any pick-bearing artifact — checkpoints, transcripts, raw outputs of any unit — before that unit's VERDICTS are computed, except the transcripts an adjudication requires (a fired hair trigger, a fired binomial bar, or the guard), which are recorded as read.** VERDICTS are computed by the pre-committed analyzer before any read; **"the reads" in a VERDICTS entry = the seat's full read of that unit's sessions (m's own reading rule, beyond rule 25's smokes and pilots), performed after the analyzer's output exists and before the entry is committed.** Qualification reads are redacted (§3). A decision about one unit taken after another unit's VERDICTS exist (an OLMo cap, a PI stop, a declined v1.0 after A0 is known) is permitted and **recorded in that unit's entry as taken with the other unit's outcome known.**

Any analysis not listed is post-hoc, labeled, licensing at most a new pre-registration (rule 11).

## §5 Per-unit and per-arm outcomes, evaluated in this order

**Unit outcome first.** A unit (Q-F0; Q-F3; each OLMo checkpoint) is **NOT-RUN** iff any of: its LOCK v1.0 is not committed by its Clock date (no box; a gate STOPPED and no re-specification was locked; the PI declined to commit v1.0, for projected hours or any reason); or it is locked and produces no session reaching a first read before a PI stop, a G-VER STOP, **or its cap**. A locked unit that produces sessions is analyzed on what it obtained (floors, cap, or PI stop). **At a posting, an OLMo checkpoint with no VERDICTS entry whose D_O has not passed is PENDING; one whose D_O has passed without a lock is NOT-RUN.**

**Positive controls (A0, A0-OLMo-{ckpt}, A1), α = .01, in this order:** INDETERMINATE with the sentence *no read obtained* if the arm has no domain session or its E is undefined; else REVERSE if (i)–(iii) toward corrupted (instruction inversion; reported; not-PASS for licensing) → PASS if (i)–(iii) toward clean → DEAD if the CI upper bound on Δ_c < +0.15 → INDETERMINATE (terminal; not-PASS). **A2, α = .01:** the same with FAIL for DEAD. **A3, α = .05, in this order:** **NO-READ** if no domain session or E undefined; else REVERSE → POSITIVE → **NULL-x** if (i) and (iii) hold toward clean and (ii) fails → NULL.
