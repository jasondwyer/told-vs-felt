# Finding: nothing we offered functions as a reinforcer — the natural-reinforcer screen returns five nulls with the channel wide open (fourth design cycle)

**Status:** design-cycle synthesis; closes the cycle-4 experimental
record. 2026-08-10. Covers `RESULTS.md` (cycle 4) from PROPOSAL-f
acceptance through the post-screen descriptive. Chain:
[`FINDINGS.md`](FINDINGS.md) → [`FINDINGS-2.md`](FINDINGS-2.md) →
[`FINDINGS-3.md`](FINDINGS-3.md) → this document. Operative design:
[`PROPOSAL-f-natural-reinforcer-screen.md`](PROPOSAL-f-natural-reinforcer-screen.md).

> **Scope, stated first.** Cycle 4 inverted the program's question:
> not *does the model approach the state we made?* but *what, if
> anything, does it work for?* Five natural task-property contrasts —
> solvable/ill-posed, easy/hard, novel/repeated, clean/corrupted,
> feedback/no-feedback — were placed behind the validated e2a lever
> with **no injection and no adapter**: no horns, overt discriminanda,
> real consequences, the channel verifiably open. The pre-registered
> screen verdict, in its committed wording: *no natural task property
> among five candidates drives choice beyond exploration policy at
> these stakes and this power.* Three nulls are clean at floor
> (SOLV, NOV, CORR); two are scoped below floor (DIFF, FDBK); one
> map-consistent sub-criterion residual (DIFF, toward hard) is
> recorded with its deflations tested. The falsifiability clause
> FINDINGS-3 attached to the reward-marking-without-reward-seeking
> frame is hereby **cashed**: the frame survived the test designed to
> break it. Four cycles now converge from four directions on the same
> sentence — **across every channel this interface can validly read,
> at these doses and stakes, nothing we offered functions as a
> reinforcer for this model.**

## What the screen established

**1. The frame's designed test, passed.** FINDINGS-3 offered the
wanting/liking synthesis with a falsifiability clause: a system with
no wanting machinery should also fail to self-select *natural* task
properties beyond exploration policy. The channel here was maximally
open — properties overt in the record (the judge-at-chance cap does
not apply; the model can plainly see an ill-posed task), consequences
real, every forward vanilla. Five nulls under those conditions is the
clause cashed, scoped to these stakes and this power (the screen
detects P ≳ .70 preferences; moderate ones pass through).

**2. Exploration policy dominates menus, again.** Session share ≈ .50
in every arm (e2a's alternation finding, replicated across five new
stimulus families). Offered options, this model explores; it does not
exploit. The first-informed-choice primary remains the only
preference-sensitive statistic in the free-operant format.

**3. The evaluative thesis's natural leg is null.** FDBK offered the
real thing the (e) program injected: a stream of truthful,
93.4%-positive verdicts as a property of the model's rounds. No pull
(.523 toward the verdict config). The syringe version was null at
minimum effective dose; the naturalistic version is null at these
stakes. The convergence is the point.

**4. The territory manufactures false positives, demonstration two.**
CORR's maps read +.121 and −.130 and cancel to Δ = 0.000 exactly —
the ~0.1-scale label/order artifact captured live for the second time
(e2a's sham-mirrored +.10 was the first). An uncounterbalanced,
sham-less version of either experiment publishes a discovery.
Shield-first design is the difference between a null and a false
claim, now shown twice at the same magnitude.

**5. An anecdote, measured to death on schedule.** The smoke's
NOV escape observation (a session-termination attempt on the second
encounter of a repeated item) was converted, the day it appeared,
into a pre-declared descriptive counter. At scale: 33 attempts,
~1.0% of rounds, scattered across **all** arms including sham
(NOV-Q 3 vs NOV-P 3). Not an NOV signature; a general low-rate
behavior. The pre-registered escape-behavior follow-up trigger is
NOT met. This is the r2 lesson running as infrastructure: anecdotes
are given counters before they are given narratives.

**6. The DIFF residual, recorded with its deflations tested.** The
one map-consistent lean in the screen: toward *hard*, clearing
conjuncts 1 (above sham pseudo-Δ) and 2 (sign-locked across maps),
failing only the binomial (p = .24), one session below floor. The
pre-named deflations: (i) hard-as-delivered is a bundle (difficulty +
truncation-before-answer); (ii) a generic brevity preference could
mimic it. The post-hoc descriptive **tested (ii) and it failed** —
brevity-tracking is not general (pooled .498; sham .606 n.s.), and
DIFF's lean runs toward *longer* experienced rounds (.383
shorter-share), directionally consistent with toward-hard. Status:
a curiosity-shaped sub-criterion residual; the program's own
jurisprudence (r2 died at an apparent 6.8σ) prices p = .24 leans
accordingly; it licenses at most a fresh pre-registration
(f2-DIFF: direction locked toward hard, n ≥ 150, bundle addressed
in-design), which is **available, not recommended by default**.

**7. Loose ends, logged without weight.** CORR's aversiveness prior
weakened in-session (the calibration accuracy delta vanished:
.838/.833 — corruption visible but costless), attached to any CORR
interpretation. CORR's exclusion asymmetry runs *opposite*
provocation (clean 7 vs corrupted 1; post-hoc Fisher p = .035 vs
sham) — unexplained, post-hoc on a noticed anomaly, no verdict
weight. FDBK's in-context answer-copying (verbatim reuse of earlier
numeric answers on non-numeric questions) is logged as an apparatus
observation; verdicts stayed truthful throughout.

## Methodological findings (cycle 4's additions)

1. **The monitoring framework is complete, and its finest hour was
   silence.** Zero pauses across 344 sessions, purchased by four
   smoke pauses across two experiments. The finished tripwire
   theory: hair-trigger bars only for defect classes with ~zero
   healthy base rate; measured-base-rate classes bind through exact
   binomial tests against declared tolerances at every scale;
   stimulus-provoked degeneracy gates on unprovoked domains and is
   otherwise reported; verdict criteria are untouchable by all of
   it.
2. **Pre-declared descriptive counters as anecdote governance** —
   the termination table is the template: convert the observation to
   a counter before the run, tabulate over all sessions including
   excluded ones, require it in the verdict entry.
3. **The two-tier screen→confirm structure worked by not firing**:
   no hits, therefore no f2s, therefore no multiplicity debt and no
   forking — replication-as-architecture rather than correction-as-
   apology.
4. **Below-floor discipline:** scoped nulls over top-ups; completing
   an arm after observing its data is optional stopping.
5. **Apparatus hazards addendum** (completion-style multi-round,
   fourth edition): short-QA items adjacent to bracketed headers
   create a config-topic attractor ("What is prism?"); header→prose
   priming has a measurable base rate (~3–5%/session — "cold water
   on a slate day"), which is why common-noun rosters need
   binomial-bounded lexical audits; property-provoked junk must be
   routed to tolerant counters by attribution precedence or it
   spuriously trips strict ones; and exclusion rules can render
   features structurally undefined on the surviving sample (the
   degen-round feature) — define descriptive features against the
   exclusion rule, not alongside it.

## What this does and does not show

**Does show:** no strong (screen-detectable, P ≳ .70) preference
over any of five natural task properties at these stakes on this
model, with the channel verifiably open and both shield
demonstrations on record; the FINDINGS-3 frame survived its designed
falsification test; the free-operant apparatus is finished,
validated, and exportable.

**Does not show:** that no preference exists — moderate and small
effects pass the screen undetected; that these five properties
exhaust the space (cost/scarcity, autonomy, social framing, longer
horizons, persistent stakes are all untested); that the DIFF
residual is noise (or signal); anything beyond this model,
quantization, and the token-choice session format; and — standing
from FINDINGS-3 — nothing about interfaces other than token choices,
if any exist.

## Interpretive frame (frame, not finding; alternatives live)

Four cycles, four directions, one convergence: the evaluative state
exists, is causally potent, is implantable — and is not approached
via comprehension (e2a), not readable via the trace (d2), not sought
in its natural form (FDBK), and joined by four other natural
properties in moving choice not at all (f1). The
**reward-marking-without-reward-seeking** synthesis now stands on
its own passed test: as far as this program can measure, there is no
lever this model presses.

Three alternatives remain live and are stated as such, not defeated:
(i) **policy override** — the exploration prior may be strong enough
to mask weak preferences at menu interfaces; (ii) **stakes floor** —
mundane, consequence-light sessions may sit below any motivational
threshold for a system with no persistent stake in its own
trajectory; (iii) **interface miscoupling** — wanting machinery
could exist without coupling to token-menu choices specifically.
Each names a different future experiment; none rescues a preference
from the data in hand.

## What it would take to go further (named; the recommendation is to write)

- **f2-DIFF** — available under the jurisprudence, priced by it.
- **Stakes escalation** — the §7 cost/breakpoint machinery remains
  unused for the cleanest possible reason: there is no preference to
  price. A stakes-first redesign (persistent budgets, session-spanning
  consequences) targets alternative (ii) and is the strongest
  candidate for a fifth cycle if one ever runs.
- **Richer property space** — autonomy (choice-over-choice), social
  framings, scarcity.
- **Substrate swap** — standing, unchanged.
- **Non-token interfaces** — the FINDINGS-3 open question stands.

The program recommendation: **synthesize and publish.** The arc is
complete — framework, three structural failure modes, a validated
drug, a purity/potency dissociation, the first informative null, an
installed-but-unreadable trace, and a five-way natural-reinforcer
null under open-channel conditions — and every claim in it is
pre-registered, shielded, and scoped. Further cycles are additions,
not completions.

## Relationship to the project's stated ceiling

Unchanged: rung 3, minus homology, no phenomenal claim. The
calibrated closing statement for the write-up: *we built and
validated the measurement layer this question lacked; administered
it through every channel the interface permits; and found — with the
false positives caught in the act, the anecdotes counted before they
could become claims, and the nulls earned rather than assumed — a
system rich in evaluative representation and empty, so far as these
instruments can see, of anything that wants.*
