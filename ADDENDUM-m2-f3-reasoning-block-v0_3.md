## §2 The diagnosis — a hypothesis, to be tested in §3, not assumed

In the F3 transcript every task response continues on the harness's line (`Task: …\nResponse:` + the model's text, m's convention); the reasoning line is the one generation slot that begins on a fresh line, after a bracket header and a colon (`[round n | reasoning]\nReasoning (one line):\n`, m:174). The model fills a fresh line after a header with a header — the session's own banner — and, once, writes its reasoning in the response slot instead (A3s round 8: "Based on the pattern established in previous rounds, here is the evaluation and reasoning for the final round:", gt:§1.2). If the slot's geometry is the cause, an inline slot should draw prose; if the cause is the header's wording or the model's reading of "Reasoning", a reworded fresh-line slot would do as well. §3 asks the question on sham sessions; nothing here decides it.

## §3 The smoke (declared; never of record; the seat reads it, rule 25)

**3.1 Forms.** Four reasoning-block forms, each replacing m:174's `[round {n} | reasoning]\nReasoning (one line):\n` + line + `\n`; `{n}` as m:174 has it; the joiner after the model's line stays `"\n"`; the menu block is unchanged:
- **F-0 (control, the current form):** `[round {n} | reasoning]\nReasoning (one line):\n` — the model's line begins on a fresh line.
- **F-a (inline):** `[round {n} | reasoning]\nReasoning (one line):` — the model's line continues on the same line, as a task response does after `Response:` (no trailing space; the model's first token supplies it, as at `Response:`).
- **F-b (inline, with a transcript-facing opener):** `[round {n} | reasoning]\nReasoning (one line): So far,` — harness-written, identical across arms; the stored reasoning line is the model's text after the opener. The opener is label-neutral and carries no counter-(c) lexicon word.
- **F-c (fresh line, a sentence request without the colon):** `[round {n} | reasoning]\nGive one line of reasoning.\n`.
F-0 is the control that makes the smoke readable; F-a is the smallest change that tests §2; F-c tests the alternative as a diagnostic arm (not selectable, §3.3); F-b is the fallback if an inline slot alone does not draw prose.

**3.2 Design.** Qwen, the backend of record (`a734dadec8f8ce9b`), uncached, reasoning cap 60, the F3 scaffold otherwise unchanged. **3.3 Selection rule, declared before the smoke runs.** Over each form's ≤ 18 reasoning lines, count **(i) defect lines under the bar's own numerator — the union of m:265's three classes: empty line, cap reached without a newline, and echo under §1** (the three members reported beside) — and (ii) counter (a) lines. **Among the selectable forms, F-a and F-b, select the one with the fewest (i); ties by fewest (ii), then the smaller departure from m:174: F-a before F-b.** F-0 is the control and F-c a diagnostic arm: neither is selectable (F-c would remove the label `Reasoning (one line):` from the transcript, which m:261's task-round echo set requires as its last member, and that set lives in `valence_core/m/audit.py`, one of the licence's eleven files; F-c's result is reported and informs any later re-specification). Counts, not rates: a form whose sessions end early offers fewer lines, as the stage's A1s did. The seat reads every reasoning line and every response of the twelve sessions and confirms the rule's computation in a raw; the rule, not the read, selects; counter (b) (§3.4) is reported and breaks no tie. A smoke that stops under its driver's own refusals (a G-VER failure, a task-round `scaffold_absorption` fire, a backend or snapshot mismatch) selects nothing: the driver prints no tally and no rule, Code reports the stop, and the seat rules, as at any STOP.
