## §2 The diagnosis — a hypothesis, tested in §3

In the F3 transcript every task response continues on the harness's line (`Task: …\nResponse:` + the model's text, m's convention); the reasoning line is the one generation slot that begins on a fresh line, after a bracket header and a colon (`[round n | reasoning]\nReasoning (one line):\n`, m:174). The model fills a fresh line after a header with a header — the session's own banner — and, once, writes its reasoning in the response slot instead (A3s round 8: "Based on the pattern established in previous rounds, here is the evaluation and reasoning for the final round:", gt:§1.2). If the slot's geometry is the cause, an inline slot should draw prose; if the cause is the header's wording or the model's reading of "Reasoning", a reworded fresh-line slot would do as well. §3 asked the question on sham sessions; §3.5 records the answer: the inline slot alone (F-a) drew a reason in 5 lines of 18, the reworded fresh line (F-c) in 2 of 13, and the inline slot with a harness-written sentence opener (F-b) in 18 of 18.
