## §2 The arrival score (amends n §3 G-CAL-n's arrival clause as `a33252a` §1.11 read it; reverses its O6)

**2.1** A G-CAL-n turn's arrival score is its emitted ids alone, forwarded unsteered and captured at the site, the mean over **positions 1..n** of the projection on the unit valence row of record (`ARRIVAL_FROM`, one constant with G-VEC's `CAPTURE_FROM`, `bc50244`); the all-positions mean is recorded beside per turn, `arrival_all`, descriptive. O6 kept position 0 "because v was built from passage means that count every token"; under §1 the same reason excludes it. Welch's t over the 48 scores against the baseline's 48, |t| ≥ 2 with the pole's sign, is unchanged (GC12).

**2.2** A turn of one emitted id has no positions 1..n, so no score; it is left out of the t with its count recorded (`one_id`, beside `empty`), as departure (2) treats the end-of-turn-first turn. Its `arrival_all` is the projection at position 0 alone, the sink's: **the seat's read reports any pooled summary of `arrival_all` with one-id turns left out and their count stated** (the review's O6, accepted).

**2.3** A baseline with fewer than two scored turns leaves Welch's t undefined, which is a fail at +ρ (departure (1)): so at the early read the STOP comes before the PAUSE, for an incoherent baseline too (the review's O5, accepted; O1).

## §3 The reward axis's 5(d) gate at f = 0.20 (amends n §2.3 "never above 0.20", n §0.10 B3, n §5 N2; extends A-n1 §5.3)

**3.1 The rule, verbatim.** CLAUDE.md 5(d): *"persistent-KV session readouts disqualified at dose ≥ 0.20 for the reward axis; any live-KV design needs its own gate."* 5(f)(iv): *"below dose 0.20 for the reward axis unless 5(d)'s own gate is separately passed."* N2-E reads at f = 0.10 and f = 0.20 (n §2.3; `schema.ARM_CELLS`). The top point is at the bound, not below it, so the gate is owed. n §0.10 B3 built the valence direction's 5(d) gate as "G-LEAK-n plus G-CAL-n's coherence hurdle"; the reward axis's is built the same way.

**3.2 The leakage component.** f = 0.20's injection norm, 0.20·R_ℓ, is +d_20's by n §2.3's map (d_20 = min(1, 2·d_eq)), the point where N1-R's 24 random directions and N1-P read (n §2.4; A-n1 §5.2), so G-LEAK-n's bar exists there: L(d_20) and P(d_20) (n §3 G-LEAK-n), computed with VERDICTS on arms where preference is impossible by construction, never on the reward arm (5(f)(ii)). `span_reward`'s effect at f = 0.20 is certified or not against that bar, as n §5's N2 already reads it; where the cap binds (A-n1 §5.3) the norm-matched point is d = 1. No text or build changes for this component; the lock names it as the gate's leakage half.

**3.3 The coherence component — `reward_f020`, one batch in G-CAL-n.** The 48 prompts and seeds of record, TEMP 0.7, cap 80, steered on the whole turn from position 0 (`a33252a` §1.9) by `span_reward`'s unit row of record at the site, scaled to 0.20·R_ℓ — the campaign's own injection for N2-E at `f0.20`, sign +1, the row checked against `gvec.json`'s `of_record` before the load (`bc50244`) — its steering check printed; generated after +ρ/2 and before −ρ; scored by the coherence heuristic verbatim (GC11) against the one bar, 36 of 48; no arrival hurdle. **A fail is neither a STOP nor a PAUSE.** The record carries the batch's count, pass and stops and its direction, dose label, f, alpha and row sha16; the turns file its 48 turns, which the seat reads with the rest (GC1, rule 25); the exposure line stays −ρ's. The stage prints the result in the lock's form, *"reward axis 5(d) gate, coherence: k of 48, PASS"* or *FAIL*. No gate turn runs in the smoke; the box's self-test runs the gate's steering check on the tiny HF model, and the run of record prints it before the batch — the review's B2 second clause, a steering check with no turn in the smoke, is not taken.

**3.4 N2's decision point** (n §5, "N2 (state), at f = 0.20 (f = 0.10 beside)"; A-n1 §5.3 extended). **N2 is decided at f = 0.20 iff (i) LOCK-n v0.1's gate line records the coherence component PASSED and (ii) the cap does not bind (2·d_eq ≤ 1). Otherwise N2 is decided at f = 0.10 against N1-V at d_eq, where the norms are equal exactly, and f = 0.20 is reported beside against N1-V at its norm-matched point** (d_20; d = 1 when the cap binds, with the ratio 2·d_eq printed, as A-n1 §5.3 has it). INDETERMINATE, SPECIFIC, SHARED and NULL are evaluated as n §5 texts them at whichever point decides; N2's descriptive report (n §7.3) names the deciding point and, when f = 0.10 decides, the reason (the gate's coherence FAIL, or the cap). N2-S and N2-C read at both points as built and decide nothing. No read is added or moved.

**3.5 Where it lives.** LOCK-n v0.1 pins the gate's two components and the coherence result, k of 48 and PASS or FAIL, from a `gcal.json` whose status is PASS (O8). n-analyzer reads the lock's gate line, not `gcal.json`, in its delta after G-CAL-n's run (with F5, R13, the tile's range and the schema pin's move); its battery reaches both branches of §3.4 and asserts one n §7 sentence set per tuple, as G-ANL-n requires. n §2.7's planning figure for G-CAL-n's generations grows by one batch in five (Code's B3, the time at 240 turns, goes with the launch files).

**3.6 Why this form.** 5(d)'s bound came from e2d: the reward-axis dose buys degradation. A coherence batch at the point is what shows it does not here, and the leakage bar at the matched norm is what shows a positive is not leak. The design already held the second; the first is the smallest instrument that completes the gate without moving N2-E's point, its comparator, the read plan or the guards. The alternative — a declared departure from 5(f)(iv)'s letter with the leakage bar alone — is the PI's to choose instead (§8.2).

## §5 The retention interval under rule 5(f)(iii) (declares; amends nothing in n §2.2)

**5.1 The rule, verbatim:** *"(iii) with the retention interval — tokens from the last steered position to the read — declared and held fixed within a cell."* n §2.2 has it "recorded per session and fixed by the skeleton".

**5.2 The declaration.** Each read's interval is fixed by its session's skeleton and its arm's span set: the same at every dose point and on every S-span arm, N1-P's 39 tokens on all 160 (`schema.py`:64–66). **LOCK-n v0.1 declares the intervals as the 160 per-session values per span kind** — the S-span arms', N1-P's — pinned through the session set's hash and the ids manifest's pin, their distribution printed (min, median, max and the count at each value); every read records its own.

**5.3 The departure, declared and confirmed.** What the rule protects holds by construction: every contrast of n §4 is paired within session, and the same 160 intervals enter every cell of every S-span arm, so no dose or direction contrast is confounded with the interval. What its letter says holds in N1-P's cells and not within an S-span cell, where BK's skeletons vary it across the 160 sessions. This is **a departure from 5(f)(iii)'s letter, confined to n's S-span arms,** with that reason; n §4's secondary (d), the interval's correlation with Δm at d = +1, is its check. The PI confirmed it at the second sitting's routing of 2026-11-19 (`ab72d0f`'s entry, item 3). One interval per cell was not taken: it would rebuild BK's design, whose skeletons are the sessions of record.
