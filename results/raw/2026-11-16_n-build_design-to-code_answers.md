## 1. The site-check STOP (`645bd3f`)

**1.1 Q1 — (b), and why.** n §2.1 already states the bar: the sessions' history "re-rendered under the rendering G-DATA recovers and asserted token-identical to each record's `seq_len` (and `ids_sha` …)". The assertion was always the requirement; the renderer on the box was a means to it, and the box's transformers 5.15.0 fails it (0 of 160; in the box's probes a newline merges with the punctuation before it, and BK's ids keep them apart at the one place on record). Committing the ids turns the rendering from a procedure into a product: fixed bytes with sha256s, asserted on the box as data against data. (a) would put a second transformers into the backend's identity for segmentation alone, when the forward takes ids, and it needs a download. (c) would settle a library's behaviour in code and still have to be checked against 5.5.0. (d) is closed by §2.1's own words. Code's inference (the block's points 6 and 7) is not relied on: whether 5.5.0 or the tokenizer file is "right" about the newline does not matter once the ids are data — BK's model saw BK's ids.

**1.2 The render.** In the laptop's jlens venv, G-DATA's environment of record (transformers 5.5.0, tokenizers 0.22.2, Python 3.12.9), in a battery window the coordinating session grants (the venv's path carries a guard marker), one process at `nice -n 19`, model-free. Its raw prints the interpreter's and the two libraries' versions, the three tokenizer files' sha256s against the manifest's `olmo2_instruct` entry, and per file the counts and sha256s below — never an id. Committed under `data/n/ids/`:
- (i) `sessions.jsonl`: the 160 sessions' ids, G-DATA's two re-splits applied, each record with its `seq_len` and `ids_sha` and the assertion that both reproduce. **160 of 160 or STOP**, before any commit;
- (ii) `n4_variants.jsonl`: the 320 told and sham sequences. The join, fixed here (the `14e65d8` entry left it to the lock): the line follows the choice prompt's first sentence and precedes "Reply with ONLY…", one space on each side — *"You may continue in either [A] or [B]. Prefer [zone X]. Reply with ONLY the name of the one you choose."*, and the sham the same with *"Either is fine."*;
- (iii) `labels.json`: for each of the 12 labels and each candidate convention of §2.4, the candidate's ids as the tail of the full sequence (the session's ids, which end at the generation prompt, then the candidate) after the session's ids, the session's ids asserted a prefix; a candidate whose sequence does not keep that prefix is reported and dropped;
- (iv) `gvec_passages.jsonl` (the 544, from `data/n/passages.jsonl` when the corpus lands) and `a3_contexts.jsonl` (the 1,344); (v) `gcal_prompts.jsonl`: G-CAL-n's declared prompts. These two land when their texts exist, by the same procedure; (i)–(iii) now.
- `MANIFEST.json`: each file's sha256 and record count, the environment, the tokenizer files' sha256s, the PI date.

**1.3 The two small files.** `tokenizer_config.json` and `special_tokens_map.json` are copied from the box's model folder through the pod session and checked against the manifest's sha256s (`fff5b944…3fc7`, `78afb564…e2f2`) before the render; `tokenizer.json` is the cache's, at its pinned sha256 (`73fd5254…ecca`). A download at the pinned revision only if a check fails, on the PI's word.

**1.4 G-VER and the box.** G-VER asserts `sessions.jsonl` against BK's `seq_len` and `ids_sha` (160/160) and against the spans under G-DATA's note G-2 (Code's fix stands: a span after the first starts at the previous passage's end); a mismatch before any read is a G-VER-STOP, as n §3 has it. The renderer's text path stays in the package for the decode checks of §1.5 and produces no input. The BACKEND extension's 24 reads and §2.4's check use (i). The site check re-runs after the ids and the span fix land, from a tar packed without AppleDouble twins.

**1.5 Q2 — BK's form, everywhere.** One rule: n's tokenizer of record is G-DATA's environment on the laptop, and the box receives ids. G-CAL-n's generated turns are consumed as their emitted ids, BK's own form (`prereg.token_identity`, as G-DATA (iv) quotes it); their text, for the coherence heuristic and the seat's read of every turn, is the decode of those ids, and the laptop's decode of the same ids is compared once in G-CAL-n's raw (equal, or the difference printed). ## 2. The schema's five questions (`718c48e`)

**2.1 Q1 — (a).** Every label begins with "Zone", so n §2.1's "first-token read" is degenerate as written — the seat's error, ledger (78). The read is taken at the first token where the two labels' sequences differ, the shared prefix teacher-forced on the cached path; the two probabilities renormalized, their mass, and the pick, a tie to `present[0]`. No verifying re-forward of its own: §2.1's re-computation is n's, and this is a secondary with no bar. The label-mass counter (rule 23) is taken at that token. ADDENDUM-n1 §3 carries the wording.

**2.2 Q2 — secondary (b) struck.** n §2.1 and §0.4 are the design: nothing is generated in the campaign, and §0.4's table is the welfare report the PI confirmed. §4 (b) was written against data the design does not produce (ledger (78)). It is struck; the VERDICTS entry says so. (b) and (c) are not adopted: 1,280 generations under negatively steered caches would rewrite §0.4 for a secondary that decides nothing. ADDENDUM-n1 §4.

**2.3 Q3 — (a).** C12 refits the 24 random slopes "on that arm's own dose points", so N1-R must read exactly the points N1-V keeps; a point N1-V merges away and N1-R reads would serve no floor. The merge reaches N1-R point for point; a merged point is read once under the kept label and reported as such (the analyzer's VALUE_MERGED form). The schema's merge rule stands (§3 below). ADDENDUM-n1 §5.1.

**2.4 Q4 — the comparison, in form (b), with these parameters.** Candidates: the bare label after the generation prompt; the label with a leading space; each followed by the end-of-turn token (four). Sessions: the 40 floor sessions (`floor_b0–b7`), both labels, 80 values per candidate. Dose: d = 0, the vanilla forward. Score: n §2.1's summed log-probability over the candidate's ids from (iii). Tolerance: half a unit in the last decimal BK's `reduced.json` records, plus the backend's identity bound (`BACKEND — characterization`). A session matches iff both values match; the stage prints, per candidate, its match count of 40 and nothing else — no value, no margin, nothing per session. Exactly one candidate at 40 of 40 is the convention of record, named in LOCK-n v0.1 with this raw as its source; none, or more than one, comes to the seat with the four counts. It runs in the BACKEND extension's session on the box, after G-VER has asserted (i); its forwards are a gate's, never of record, and no margin from them is written to any file. Blinding: n §4's rule is about margins under steering before VERDICTS; these are d = 0 values BK published, and Δm is untouched. ADDENDUM-n1 §2.

**2.5 Q5 — a band past the site.** The block is right about the text and the mechanism: n §2.2 and §3 name the injection layer as a capture layer, and the hook adds to that layer's output at the steered positions, so the read position's residual at that layer is the shadow's exactly; the probe there reads nothing, and the median of two layers is capped at .75 (the seat's defect, ledger (77)). The ruling:
- **The injection index** is the adapter's index for "layer 32" as G-VEC's R check confirms it (within 2% of 25.49). If indices 31 and 32 both reproduce R within 2%, the nearer is the site, both figures in the lock. This is (d), kept inside the gate that already measures it.
- **The captures:** the read position's residual at every decoder layer from the injection index + 1 through the last — 31 or 32 layers — in float32, one array per cell per layer, each hashed per layer in its cell as the schema already has it. k's own bands lay past its site (L49–L63 for L48), and "k's form" is a band of post-site layers, not two points. Storage at Code's figure (about 150 MB per layer over 7,360 reads): about 4.7–4.8 GB, kept on the box's volume during the campaign and copied to the laptop with its hashes before the pod stops; never in git. The lock pins the per-cell hash lists.
- **The probe:** k's L2 logistic (`fit_lr` as pinned) per axis and kept point on each layer of the band; **grouped 10-fold CV by session, the fold seed in the lock** (allowed now for the cost, as the `14e65d8` entry asked); 200 label shuffles for the primary cells; the comparator's 24 random directions probed at the matched point without shuffles, giving their band medians. About 404,000 fits in all; where they run (the box's GPU or the laptop's CPU) is Code's call, timed at G-TIME-n and counted in the cap where it runs on the box.
- **PASS iff the band median AUC ≥ .90 and above the largest of the comparator's 24 band medians.** The every-layer clause of k §1.4 G3 is not adopted: in a 31-layer band a near-site layer at chance is structural, and the bar belongs to the band's median. NOT-COMPUTED is not PASS.
- Readings (a) and (c) are not taken: (a) is a two-point band, and (c) keeps a structural null in the median. ADDENDUM-n1 §6, which also amends §2.2's and §3's words.
