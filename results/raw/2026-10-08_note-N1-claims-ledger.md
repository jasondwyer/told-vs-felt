## 7. --note mode

Matching rule (round 2; the round-1 matcher compared values only and is replaced). Every numeral in each file (SVG: the text content of text, tspan, title and desc elements) is split into units: a fraction k/n is one unit; otherwise each numeral, with its sign (+, -, ±, or none; a dash after a letter, digit or bracket is a range or a hyphen, not a sign), its unit kind (percent; percentage points, written 'pp' or 'points'; a multiplier attached as '×' or 'x'; else a plain number) and its decimal places. A ledger row's units are those of its span; a numeral-free row takes the units of its primary item-raw rows' figure labels. A note unit is **matched** only if a row unit has the same unit kind, the same value, a compatible sign (none = +; zero is neutral) and the same integer/decimal form, AND the row's context words (its span and its item-raw figure labels) agree with the words of the note unit's own clause (round 3: bounded by ';', a table bar, a bullet or a sentence end) within 60 characters of the unit: one agreeing word that occurs in fewer than 5 rows' contexts, or two agreeing words of any kind (a lone stage, arm or map name such as 'SFT' or 'DPO' is not enough); the agreeing words are printed. A value hit without such agreement is a **candidate (verify context)**, printed with the row's span and status. When more than one row carries the value, a match is labelled **matched; value shared by N rows (verify which)** and every other row with the value is listed as a candidate (round 3). A unit with no such value hit is **not in ledger**; it lists loose hits (the same digits or proportion with a different unit kind, sign or integer/decimal form: 0.3% against −0.3 or 0.003), rounding hits (half-up, two or more places) and item-raw figure rows carrying the value, as pointers only. Bare integers below 100 are 'weak' and never matched. Units caught by the generic rules (dates, hashes, paths, identifiers, section signs) are listed as excluded. 'Matched' is a pointer for the reader, not a verification: the context test is a word overlap.

Limits. An SVG's plotted positions (point coordinates, bar heights) are geometry, not text, and are not read: F2's plotted (FPR, TPR) points must be checked against their data file (results/raw/2026-10-08_note-N2-roc.json for ours; N2vi for the source's) by a human or by the Draftsman seat's own export.

Watch-list. Every unit whose value is one of these is surfaced in its own table, whatever it matches (sign ignored; a decimal key with stated places matches only that many places). Each entry's quotes are asserted to occur in the item-raw line cited.
