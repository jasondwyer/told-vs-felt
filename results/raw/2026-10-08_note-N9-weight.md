### 2a. Post-hoc observations on the bound (labeled post-hoc; they change no gate)

1. **Resolution.** Every stored leak summary is consistent with per-text |Delta| on a 1/16-logit grid:
   - A: max x 16 = 28.0000 (integer: True); n x mean x 16 = 493.9776, nearest integer 494, residual -0.0224 within the 4-dp rounding tolerance +/-0.0256: True
   - B: max x 16 = 17.0000 (integer: True); n x mean x 16 = 269.9776, nearest integer 270, residual -0.0224 within the 4-dp rounding tolerance +/-0.0256: True
   - A_smoke: max x 16 = 8.0000 (integer: True); n x mean x 16 = 124.0064, nearest integer 124, residual 0.0064 within the 4-dp rounding tolerance +/-0.0256: True
   This is what label logits held at bfloat16 precision would produce if the label logits are of magnitude >= 8 (the gate casts to float32 only after the unembedding, d_gates.py line 111, local). The bf16 spacing, computed by this run at representative magnitudes, is 0.03125 at 4, 0.03125 at 6, 0.0625 at 8, 0.0625 at 12, 0.125 at 16, 0.125 at 24, 0.25 at 32: 1/32 on [4, 8), 1/16 on [8, 16), 1/8 on [16, 32), so a sum or difference of bf16 logits that are each of magnitude >= 8 is a multiple of 1/16, and one involving a logit below 8 need not be. It is consistent with that, not proof of it. On such a grid the 0.10 bar admits at most one grid step (0.0625) per text, and the observed maxima are 28 steps (A) and 17 steps (B), so the resolution does not touch the verdict.
