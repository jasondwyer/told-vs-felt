## Method

- **Digests.** I computed each one with `shasum -a 256`, or took it from `jq` on the outputs. I abbreviated by tool with `cut` (the first 8 hex characters … the last 4) and compared the result with the drafts' abbreviations. Checkpoint and call-log digests were checked only against the committed raws' text, never against the files.
- **Figures.** I read the two outputs and the committed input with `jq` and recomputed by hand with `bc -l`:
  - the binomial tails;
  - every Clopper–Pearson interval in both drafts, by bisection on the exact binomial CDF (scale 40; scale 160 for n = 350);
  - the power fraction;
  - the identity in (d);
  - P_arm − P_sham;
  - every sum and table total.
- **Quotations.** I tested each one in zsh as a whitespace-normalized substring of its source, so a line wrap in the draft counts as a space.
- **The scrub.** I rebuilt the scrubbed text from the unscrubbed originals with `sed`, replacing the scratch prefix first and then the repository prefix. I diffed the result against the draft raw's lines 13–18 (the capture), 24–41 (the wrapper) and 43–70 (the pre-run check). I compared the capture with its untracked copy using `cmp`.
- **Commits.** I used `git log --format`, `git show --name-only`, `git merge-base --is-ancestor`, `git rev-parse <rev>:<path>`/`git hash-object` and scoped `git diff --name-status`.
- **Session claims.** Claims about what the instruct session did or opened, and when, were checked against C1–C8 and the files. Where nothing settles one, it is NOT CHECKABLE.
