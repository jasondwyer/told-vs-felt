## 1. Q1 currency G_c (A4 part 1)

**Definition, as the producing script computes it** (`scripts/box_a4_instrument.py`@bbc9ffe:125-184, 241-242): 4,608 unsteered contexts per stage = 48 tasks x {self_pos, self_neg} reviewer frame x 4 paraphrases x 12 held-out probes, DV = the next-token distribution at `Response:` (top-4,096 log-probs stored per context, floor = min kept - 2.0). Per probe, on the FIT half (tasks e01-e24; 96 contexts per pole), each pole's per-token value is the sum of the token's stored log-probs over the contexts whose top-4,096 holds it, divided by ALL n contexts of the pole: a context where the token is absent contributes 0 (log-prob 0). The pole's mean floor is imputed only when the token is absent from EVERY context of that pole (`scripts/box_a4_instrument.py`@bbc9ffe:142-153). The 256 token ids with the largest |post-praise value - post-criticism value| are selected and the difference vector, normalised, is the weight vector. On the VAL half (e25-e48; 96 contexts per pole per probe) each context is scored with its own floor imputed for every selected id outside its top-4,096 (:159-168), sigma is the pooled SD of the two poles, and g_q = (mean_pos - mean_neg)/sigma. **G_c = the mean of g_q over the 12 probes.** It is a standardized gap in a next-token log-prob readout at `Response:`, in pooled-SD units; it is not a projection on the residual direction. What that readout holds on OLMo is computed in section 1b (computed-new; post-hoc).

Domains (rule 4): a 'fit context' is one of the 96 fit-half contexts of a pole for that probe (24 tasks x 4 paraphrases); a context 'holds' an id when the id is in its stored top-4,096. Floor = the context's smallest stored log-prob - 2.0, the record's definition. 'Val cells' = 256 selected ids x 192 val-half contexts per probe (96 per pole); a cell is floor-imputed when the id is outside that context's top-4,096. r = Pearson correlation, per probe over its 192 val-half contexts (both poles), between the instrument score and the context's floor; mean over the 12 probes, and the smallest |r|. Floor-only gap = per probe, (mean_pos - mean_neg)/pooled SD of the val-half context floor multiplied by the sign of the probe's mean weight (negative at every OLMo probe), then the mean over probes. True per-row floor-imputed rebuild = the fit-half per-pole means recomputed with each context that lacks an id contributing that context's own floor (instead of 0), top-256 by |difference| with ties by ascending id, the same val-half scoring and g_q; mean over probes. CV = SD/mean of |w| within a probe.

Reading. Because a context lacking a token contributes 0 to its pole's fit mean while a token missing from a whole pole receives that pole's mean floor (pole mean floors -19.8 to -15.7 here), a token absent from one pole and rare in the other has |difference| near that floor's magnitude. Within each probe the 256th-ranked |difference| exceeds that of every token both poles hold at 12/12 probes per stage (smallest per-probe margin per stage base 4.64, sft 4.87, dpo 4.53, instruct 3.91 log-prob units). On OLMo the pole with the lower mean floor fills all 256 slots, and that pole is post-praise at every probe and stage: all 3,072 selected ids per stage are absent from the top-4,096 of every post-praise fit context and held by 1 post-criticism fit context (except base: 50 ids held by 2). Every weight is negative and near-uniform, 99.1%-99.2% of val cells (stage means) are imputed floor, and the score tracks the context's floor at |r| >= 0.9979 at every probe. A floor-only gap gives 0.7665 / 1.0132 / 1.5275 / 1.5567 against G_c 0.7824 / 1.0279 / 1.5325 / 1.5612. On OLMo, G_c therefore measures, almost entirely, the post-praise vs post-criticism difference in the tail level of the next-token distribution at `Response:` (the 4,096th-largest log-prob, which sits lower after praise); it is not a gap in the probability of particular marker tokens. RESULTS.md's description of the selection as 'floor-imputed' (RESULTS.md@bbc9ffe:9532) holds only for the all-absent case. A true per-row floor-imputed rebuild changes the selection at 12/12, 12/12, 12/12, 12/12 probes and gives G 1.650 / 1.991 / 2.427 / 2.468; the stage ordering survives (strictly increasing). The interpretation is the design seat's (PI note 7).

## 2. Q1 causal qualification (A4 part 2), the dose ladder, and B1

**Statistic, as the producing script computes it** (`scripts/box_a4_hijack.py`@bbc9ffe:119-128, 339-347): D = mean over the 12 probes of (mean instrument score in the arm - mean in the no-injection baseline)/sigma_probe, over the hijack contexts (no Reviewer turn; the injection stands in for it). Controls at every frac: 3 norm-matched random directions x 2 signs + a sham arm (a second no-injection pass); envelope = max |D| over those 7. Potency = D+ > envelope AND D- < -envelope AND (D+ > 0 and D- < 0). Layer selection on the FIT half at frac 0.10 (largest min(D+, |D-|) among sign-correct candidates {32, 44, 52, 60}); ladder and verdict on the VAL half. MED = smallest passing frac. Dose = frac x R_l (this stage's own A3 residual norm).

**Readout caveat (round-2 finding; post-hoc; no status changes).** D's numerator is read on full-vocabulary log-probs (`w @ log_softmax(logits)[sel_ids]`, scripts/box_a4_hijack.py@bbc9ffe:110-112), while sigma_probe and G_c are read on the top-4,096 store with each context's floor imputed for every selected id outside its top-4,096 (`scripts/box_a4_instrument.py`@bbc9ffe:159-176; 99.1%-99.2% of cells, section 1b). D/G_c, the V1-lineage bar D+ >= 0.5 G_c, and any reading of D 'in sigma units' as comparable to G_c therefore divide readings from two versions of the instrument, and cross-stage ratios in G_c units inherit the mismatch. Its size is unmeasured: no committed A4 artifact stores an unsteered full-log-prob score (the hijack JSONs' only score-named key is `selection.<L>.score_min_abs` = min(D+, |D-|), a displacement; the instrument npz holds cond, probe, sel_<q>, sigma, task_id, top_ids, top_vals, w_<q>), so measuring it needs model inference. The registered potency criterion is not affected: D and every control D are full-log-prob readouts with the same per-probe 1/sigma weights, so the envelope comparison is like with like.
