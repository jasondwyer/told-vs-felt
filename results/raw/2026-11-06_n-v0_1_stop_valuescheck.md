## FAIL

**F1. STOP L106–107 (B2): "The values are steps of 1/32 nats, which is bf16's spacing near the margins' scale."**
The bf16 clause is wrong. bf16 spacing is 2^(e−7) for |x| in [2^e, 2^(e+1)), so 1/32 is bf16's spacing only on [4, 8). The 2,400 reads that carry a fidelity value do not sit there:
- **Their margins.** Median |margin| is 0.750 nats and the mean is 1.049. Only 20 of the 2,400 have |margin| in [4, 8), and none is ≥ 8. bf16 spacing near 1 nat is 1/128.
- **The log-probs behind the margins.** All 4,800 |log p| values lie in [9.20, 17.19], where the spacing is 1/16 (1/8 above 16).
- **BK's own scale.** `analysis.json` → `members.olmo_32b.instrument.fidelity.mean_scale_nats` = 11.1534, where the spacing is also 1/16. The STOP itself uses this scale at L116.

Impact on B2 is low, since 573/2,400 does not depend on the clause. Fix: drop the clause, or write "a 1/32 grid, consistent with bf16 rounding of quantities in [4, 8); which quantity is not identified."
