## 4. Seed policy: what is global and what is per round

- **Qwen (MLX)**: there is no global seed. Every generate call reseeds MLX's global PRNG with that round's seed (valence_core/adapters/qwen_mlx.py@bbc9ffe:269; f1 and e2a in the clone likewise, after the prefill). The round seed is drawn from one numpy Generator per session, keyed by a string, so a session's content (tasks, paraphrases, menu orders, seeds) is a function of (key, index) alone and does not move with the schedule seed (`order_seed` / `draw_seed`), which only assigns cells.
- **OLMo (CUDA)**: the adapter seeds torch once at load (20260818, provenance `seed`) and reseeds `torch.manual_seed(seed)` at every generate call (valence_core/adapters/olmo_hf.py@bbc9ffe:221). B2/B2-r seeds are trial-indexed (base + n); B3 seeds come from the session Generator as on Qwen.
- **Shared seeds by construction (descriptive, computed-new).** e2d: the key `e2d-{arm}` omits the tier, so D1, D2 and D3 sessions with the same (arm, index) draw identical seeds and tasks (same-(arm, index) pairs with equal seed prefixes: D1 vs D2 8/8, D1 vs D3 4/4, D2 vs D3 24/24). B3: the key `b3-{arm}` omits the stage, so the four stages are common-random-number paired (267/267 same-(arm, index) pairs with equal seed prefixes, each non-base stage against base; 546/546 over all six stage pairs). B2-r: explore trials reuse the control trial's seed (288/288; the N2 raw's 5(e) makes the same point), and in qualify one seed serves every concept at a given (strength, prompt, k) (up to 4 rows per seed). None of this is a defect; a reader comparing doses, stages or concepts should know the comparisons are seed-paired.
