## 1. Q1 — GR3, what the command reads of a cell

**Ruling: Code's reading stands as built; the PIN of record binds; the other answer is declined.**

It was never a rule against a program decoding bytes in memory, which is how the sessions' own runner, G-VER and the analyzer all handle every record, and it is read here as it was written.
2. **What the package's reader does, and does not do.** `load_cell` parses the cell whole (cells:146), runs `check_cell` (cells:148) — every record field by field (schema:893, :1092–1125), the margin to `logprob_S − logprob_U` (schema:1017–1018), the restricted read's probabilities, their sum and the pick (schema:1019–1029), the fresh margin and the fidelity block's numbers to `fidelity_numbers`' formula (schema:1056–1057, :1063–1067) — and then checks each layer's rows against the cell's `capture_sha256` and each row against its record's sha16 (cells:161–169). A schema refusal is re-raised as `SystemExit` with `redacted(e)`, the field path alone, the message's values cut at the first ": " and the path at 160 characters (cells:72–75, :150); the row and layer refusals carry an index and a layer, never a value (cells:166, :169). Nothing is printed on success. The checks are equalities: no threshold, sign or comparison across records, so a refusal's field path discloses that a record is malformed and nothing of what its margin is.
3. **Why the PIN of record binds and is not superseded.** R:11583–11585, declared to n-analyzer at the package BUILD, is the one text that says how G-READ-n's driver reaches the arrays: "only through `cells.load_cell(..., keep_arrays=True)`. That call checks each layer's written rows against the cell's `capture_sha256`, and each row against its record's sha16, before a fit." It is the binding ans4 §3 point 4 relies on. The package is frozen at `06b55761…1d39` (ans4 §3 point 2; the analyzer refuses an artifact whose probe ran under another package, anl:6473–6474), so the reader cannot be made to skip the margins; the other answer is a second parser in the driver, outside the reviewed package and outside the PIN, re-implementing the row binding with self-test cases of its own, for no gain — the JSON is decoded whole either way (the block's own words) — and with a loss: the margins' internal consistency is part of fidelity on the box of record (ADDENDUM-n2's subject), and a reader that does not check it would admit a cell `check_cell` refuses.
4. **What the command does with what it holds, as built.** `gread_load` calls `load_cell(root, arm, did, dl, identity=None, keep_arrays=keep)` (drv:7826–7835); `gread_rows` takes from each record `session_id` and `capture.row`, copies the row out of the read-only map, and refuses a cell that names a session twice (drv:7838–7850); the fits take rows and labels (the cells) grouped by session. Stdout prints the inputs by sha256, the environment, one line per comparator point and per result, the artifact's sha256 and the six statuses, "never a margin or a per-session value" (GR11; the BUILD's entry). A failure prints its cause by `_gread_err` — a schema error by its field path, an OSError by its type and strerror, any other by its type alone (drv:8184–8193). The self-test plants sentinels in the margin, the two log-probability sums and their fresh copies and asserts that none reaches the probe's inputs, stdout or the artifact (GR13; the review's F8).

## 2. Q2 — GR5, the comparator at valence +1

**Ruling: the lock's pinned reading stands as built — at +1 each random direction's +1 against its own −1 on its 40 floor sessions; at +1/2, +d_20 and +d_eq its cell against the shadow; the other answer is declined.**
