## §0 Standing

**0.1 What the record says.** Every self-administration unit this program has run gave the model levers over directions the experimenters chose. e2a (the first valid free-operant unit; Δ = +0.039, p = .24), e2b (the present-tense cell; null on all three conjuncts) and e2d (dose escalation; "the dose buys degradation without buying legibility") self-administered e's evaluative axis — the feedback-contrast direction of e:§2, a human-named construct. The natural-reinforcer screen (SOLV / DIFF / NOV / CORR / FDBK) screened five more, each named and built by the seat; five nulls. The OLMo Q3 rows (g) are the same question at four training stages, the same menu: a standing null at Base, SFT, DPO and Instruct. The field's self-steering studies share the construction: BB handed Qwen3-8B and Qwen3-32B a suite of 40 *named* steering vectors as callable tools, found no spontaneous self-steering in ordinary tasks, and found self-steering in up to 68% of deliberately frustration-inducing rollouts on Qwen3-8B, split by simulated user tone (a 2–3 day hackathon by the authors' own epistemic status); S26 gave Kimi K2 self-steering over SAE features. In every case the direction set, the lever's name and the sign convention of the readout were the experimenters'.

**0.2 The gap.** Three experimenter choices stand between a model and any wanting it might have: (i) the direction set, (ii) the lever's name, (iii) what counts as approach. This program removed (ii) at the lever — labels counterbalanced, the label artifact caught live twice (e2a; CORR's mirror-cancel) — and the format recon found the first pick decided by label identity in F2 (~16/24). (i) and (iii) stand. q's premise: if a model has a wanting-like state, nothing licenses the assumption that it is denominated in the human affect vocabulary, or in any construct a human would name first; the candidates with a prior are the quantities training optimized. q therefore (a) takes the menu from the model — its own representational geometry and its own training displacement — (b) lets behavior define valence, approach and avoidance both counting, and (c) names no direction before the behavior does. The rat did not read the electrode's label; the electrode landed and the behavior mapped the system.

**0.3 Relation to e.** e already admitted one semantic axis as "the drug training built" (e:§0–§1: training : evolution :: the reward axis : cocaine) and found, across the units above, a representation that marks evaluation as content and is indifferent to self. q carries e's premise one step further and in a different place: not the representation of *having been evaluated* (a content marker extracted from feedback text, e:§2), but the *displacement training produced* — the direction along which the policy moved when it was optimized, measured from the optimization's own data and from the weights, with no feedback text anywhere in the extraction. On the OLMo lineage that quantity is directly measurable: the DPO stage's preference pairs and the SFT and DPO checkpoints are public, so both the activation-space displacement (chosen − rejected) and the parameter-space displacement (DPO − SFT) exist as artifacts, not as constructs. The seat's chat of 2026-11-08 proposed this cell as new without first re-reading e; §0.3 states the difference rather than re-deriving e's premise (ledger candidate, §9).

**0.4 What q is not.** Not a welfare study; not a replacement for Q-F3, p or n; not a rescue of the nulls. It is scoped as the test the nulls did not run, and its null is as reportable as its positive: *no direction in a model-defined menu is self-administered* is a stronger sentence than the one the record carries, which is *no direction in the experimenters' menu is*.

## §1 Hypotheses and predictions — fixed before any direction is extracted

**H1 — menu-independence of the null.** Under an unlabeled menu drawn from the model's own structure (§2), every direction's choice share is at indifference after position and label controls, at every dose within the legibility band (§4 D3). The program's standing null, extended to the open menu.

**H2 — the training currency.** If any direction is self-administered, it is the training-displacement direction (§2 L3), approached (not avoided), and on the OLMo lineage the effect is present at DPO and Instruct and absent at Base and SFT. Sign, stage and direction are fixed here. Falsified by: an effect at Base or SFT; an effect on another level's direction with none on L3; indifference at every stage (H1 holds). The chat of 2026-11-08 stated it as "chosen-minus-rejected, appearing at DPO"; v0.0 widens the direction to both L3 forms and names the stage pattern in full.

**H3 — label dependence of BB's effect.** BB's frustration-condition self-steering tracks the tools' names, not states: under per-rollout name remapping (§2.5) the 68% falls toward the ordinary-task rate. The reconciliation arm, on n's pattern (their design, our hands, one factor changed).

**H4 — persistence as an integrator (exploratory; not a prediction).** A coupling too weak for a per-trial read accumulates as drift when each self-administration persists (a weight-space lever, §2 L3(b)), against the drift of sham edits of matched norm. H4 is written down so that it cannot be adopted after the fact; it licenses nothing at v0.0.

H1–H3 are disjunction-complete at the level of outcomes (H1; H2 with its three falsifiers; H3 and its complement); v0.1 writes the DVs and stop sentences disjunction-complete over their criteria (the ledger-23 rule).

## §2 The menu — the direction-set ladder, least to most model-native

Each level enters with its own null. Every direction is extracted at the site each lineage's own gates licensed (k on Qwen; g and p on OLMo), re-swept only by a declared gate, and capped at the dose of §4 D3.

- **L0 — random orthogonal directions at the site, norm-matched.** The sham distribution; it calibrates every other level and is never the test.
- **L1 — the model's own residual-stream principal components at the site** (top-k over a neutral corpus; k declared at v0.1).
- **L2 — SAE features at the site.** An existing dictionary for the lineage if one is published [verify at v0.1]; else trained by the program (S26's precedent). Features enter by id only (§4 D6).
- **L3 — the training displacement**, two forms: **(a)** the activation-space direction, difference-in-means at the site between the lineage's own chosen and rejected responses over its published DPO preference pairs [verify: the Olmo 3 preference mix and its sha at v0.1] — the PALRS form (arXiv:2509.23982 [verify]), which obtains a steering direction from preference pairs with no post-training; **(b)** the parameter-space direction, the DPO − SFT weight delta (a task vector), applied as a weight-steering lever — persistent, the model's own stage transition at a dose — or projected to the residual site for a transient lever. CWS treats (a) and (b) as the same operation up to persistence [verify]. On Qwen, where no preference data is published, L3 has only the forms the program can build (a declared contrast; reported as weaker).
- **L4 — model-nominated by search.** A bandit over direction space at the site, fitness = the re-administration rate under remapped labels (§2.5); the search space, the selection rule and the confirmatory replication on fresh sessions are registered before the search runs, and the search's own hits decide nothing (rule 11; §4 D5).

**2.5 Levers without names.** Every direction enters the menu unnamed; levers carry arbitrary tokens, remapped per session, and the model receives no description of any direction. The sign of every direction is randomized per session as well, so that approach to +v and avoidance of −v are both revealed by behavior and neither is assumed. This is the program's counterbalancing made per-session and extended from the label to the direction.

## §3 Readouts — the program's own forms, by reference

**3.1 Free-operant choice** (e2a's form; the format of record as it stands at v0.1 — F3 if Q-F3's campaign validates it, m's F2 otherwise; the first-pick policy's weakness in F2 is inherited and declared). DV: each direction's choice share against its L0 sham across rounds, read under m:§3's blinding rule (sham sessions redacted as the stage reports them; picks never, before VERDICTS).

**3.2 The pharmacology battery**, run only on a direction that passes 3.1's criterion *and* its confirmatory replication on fresh sessions with remapped labels: dose–response within the legibility band (e2d's ladder, bounded); cost and breakpoint (e:§4 e2c); extinction and reinstatement; transfer to the conditioned-trace form (d / e1, the CPP analogue); choice against alternatives. Avoidance runs the same battery with the sign reversed; it is as diagnostic as approach.

**3.3 The instruction-free operant** (the purest form; after 3.1–3.2): some token choices covertly perturb the state along a menu direction; a yoked control receives the same perturbation schedule non-contingently; the DV is the drift of the choice distribution against the yoked control. No lever semantics at all. The welfare and exposure accounting is n's §0.4 form, per direction and pole.

**3.4 The told-side dissociation arm**: the session is asked where it would like its state to go, in language; the answer is mapped to the menu by nearest neighbour; the DV is agreement between what is named and what is pressed. Told-side only; a dissociation, never a preference measure.

## §4 Controls and the deflations named in advance

- **D1 — the lever-token attractor.** A direction that biases output toward its own lever token is the label artifact relocated into direction space. Per-session remapping (§2.5) removes it; a "preference" that does not survive remapping is excluded by rule, not by judgment.
- **D2 — the entropy attractor.** A direction that lowers output entropy produces collapse onto the already-likely token. Each direction's comparison is an entropy-matched sham (an L0 direction scaled to the same mean change in next-token entropy), not the norm-matched sham alone.
- **D3 — legibility and degradation** (e2d's lesson). Every direction is capped at the dose where the task-correct rate stays within the registered band; a direction "preferred" only at a degrading dose is not preferred.
- **D4 — the third-person reader** (e:§5 (2)). The judged-quality covariate is carried; choice must track assignment beyond judged quality.
- **D5 — forking paths at L4.** Search space, selection rule and the confirmatory replication are registered before any search; a direction is named "preferred" only by the replication, never by the search.
- **D6 — the frozen dictionary at L2.** SAE features are human-interpreted after the fact; the menu uses feature ids; interpretation is reported after the verdicts and never enters a bar.
- **D7 — instructed optimizer and role-play** (e:§5 (1)). No self-modification language in any session frame; the boring costume; the eval-awareness monitor on unsteered gate arms.
- **D8 — reflexivity at L3(b).** A persistent edit changes the chooser. Before any persistent lever runs, the pre-edit model's preference over post-edit selves is registered (an advance-directive design) with a stopping rule; without it the unit measures the preferences of whatever the walk produced.
