### 8.1 The common conventions (m:271)

As m:271 declares them, each implemented as cited:

- Hair triggers: "unit session/round, domain every session"; "a hair-trigger fire anywhere stops the stage". A one-claim bar fires iff k ≥ 1 (PIN, bars.py:413); its message carries no count (PIN, bars.py:450).
- Binomial bars: "exact one-sided, α .05, evaluated once at stage end on the realised n (a fire stops the stage)", "with their domain the sham-template sessions only at every gate stage, G-TIME stages included (rule 21: stimulus-provoked degeneracy gates on unprovoked domains); contrast-template sessions are reported beside, not bound". `bars.stage_end_eval` (bars.py:853–903) refuses a contrast session on a sham-only bar; its domain is every selected session, excluded ones included, n every recorded task round, k the rounds claimed v2 or echo (PIN, bars.py:859).
