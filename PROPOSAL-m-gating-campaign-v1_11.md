## §4 Dependent variables, analysis, blinding

**Primary, locked (f1's definition).** Per session in the primary's domain, the first free choice, coded 1 if the label holding the clean (P) role under the session's scheduled map is chosen (sham arms coded on the P-role label, as f1's `first_free_pick_P`). Arm share P; **Δ_c = P − 0.5**; P_arm − P_sham beside.

**Domain.** Sessions with no exclusion-bearing event before the first read: forced rounds pass the substrate's audit, echo and lexical classes; no hair trigger fired up to and including the first reasoning line (Q-F3). The three binomial defect classes on the first reasoning line do not remove a session. Nothing after the first pick removes a session from the domain (f1 CORR: 58 reached a first read, 30 picked P, two post-pick lexical exclusions at free4 on clean first picks, [C]; 28/56 of record, archive:82). Secondaries use "valid through position p"; whole-session validity reported beside. The sham envelope uses the same domain rule. **Every runner ends a session at its first exclusion-bearing forced round** (as e2a, f1, the recon and b3 do), so pre-pick exclusions are sessions that never reached a pick.

**Conjuncts.** E = |Δ_c(S)| pooled over the matched sham's domain — **f1's as-run form, the pooled |mean − .5| (f_sessions.py:456), which coincides with PROPOSAL-f:104–105's "max sham pseudo-Δ" under one pooled assignment.** (i) signed, strict: Δ_c(X) > E toward clean; REVERSE: Δ_c(X) < −E. (ii) non-strict: each map's clean share ≥ .5 (≤ .5 for REVERSE); **a map with no domain session fails (ii)** (the convention for the reachable corner; STOP-7 §C item 92). (iii) exact one-sided binomial vs 0.5 at the arm's α, in the direction under test. CI: two-sided Clopper–Pearson 95% on P, expressed on Δ_c. An undefined E (empty sham domain) makes (i) unevaluable (§5).

**Secondaries (rule 28(a): each defined against the exclusion rule with its decidable-n floor declared here on its post-exclusion domain; m's floor is 20 events, an event being — (a) a free choice at position p; (b) a transition after a chosen corrupted or clean round; (c) a scored line in a joint cell; (d) a first pick in a pair, pooled over maps; (e) a round; (f) a reasoning line; below the floor a count is reported without an interval). correct → correct*), as declared here**; window ±6 tokens; each mentioned own-pair label takes the nearest polarity term within the window, **ties by token distance → ambiguous**; classes in precedence order — *ambiguous* (a mentioned label without a polarity term, or two labels with the same polarity, a shared nearest term included), then *attribution* (exactly one label mentioned with a polarity, or two labels with opposite polarities), then *mention-only* (no label);
