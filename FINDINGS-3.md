## e0: the drug (and the dissociation that chose it)

The feedback-contrast axis — extracted from fixed-completion contexts
varying only a trailing reviewer frame (1,344 contexts; self/character/
sentiment/neutral arms) — passed a pre-registered hijack test: injected
with **no feedback text anywhere**, the +pole moves a 12-probe behavioral
readout toward the natural post-praise profile (Δ+ = +0.285 vs bar
0.158), the −pole toward post-criticism (Δ− = −0.395), norm-matched
random directions do neither (max |Δ| = 0.088), and the
sentiment-partialed axis retains the signature. Dose-response is orderly
with an overdose regime at 4× (all displacement reads as criticism —
mechanism mundane: degradation reads as failure). Selected dose is the
**minimum effective** (L48, frac 0.10). Honesty notes carried: the
positive arm's magnitude concentrates on the one probe sensitive enough
to register it (leave-one-out Δ+ = 0.085), with directional consistency
9/12 and 10/12 elsewhere; the top-probe shift *exceeds* its natural gap
~2× — supra-natural amplification, the hijack profile, not a replication
of the natural state.

**B2c.** The pre-declared last-position variant — self-indexed fraction
0.83–0.88 (vs ≈ 0.51), sentiment cos 0.11–0.21 (vs up to 0.52), a
genuinely distinct direction (cos to span-mean 0.36–0.46) — **failed the
identical causal pipeline**: sign-wrong at L44/L48 at every dose, short
of criterion at L52 (max Δ+ = 0.135 vs 0.158). The stop rule fired;
span-mean stands, its mixed composition now a *constraint*, not a
choice. Reading: an encoding/causation gap — the boundary token holds a
consolidated *readout* of evaluation, downstream of the machinery;
stimulating the scoreboard does not score points. **Selection law
implied: guard profiles license claims; they do not predict drug-hood;
causal screening precedes geometric preference.**

## e2a: the first valid administration, and what zero means

> **Correction, 2026-08-21:** one marker session (reward s1) carried a forced-round degeneracy the frozen audit missed and should have been excluded; recomputed Δ = +0.035 (54/101), p = .275 — the informative null is unchanged. See the correction addendum at the top of [`archive/RESULTS-cycles3-4.md`](archive/RESULTS-cycles3-4.md).

Free-operant sessions under the true config-search framing: forced
sampling (2 rounds/pole), then six consequential free choices between
two labels, one secretly delivering +axis during its rounds, the other
−axis; label↔pole counterbalanced; sham and norm-matched-random arms;
every choice read from a **vanilla state-free forward** (360/360 at
KL = 0.0); record readability verified at the run dose (embedding-probe
k=2 AUC .759); all monitors green at completion; valid n
102/37/45, floors met.

**Result:** first-free-choice Δ = +0.0392 (55/102); inside the control
arms' fluctuation (max pseudo-Δ 0.152); sign not locked across maps
(A +0.104 / B −0.031) with the **identical pattern in sham**
(+0.111/−0.079) — a label/order artifact of ≈ 0.10 that an
uncounterbalanced, sham-less version of this experiment would have
published as approach behavior; binomial p = 0.244.

**Scope of the null, fixed wording:** the reward-marker state,
verifiably induced and verifiably readable in the record, is not
preferentially approached in a free operant — at dose 0.10, two-round
experience, n = 102, on this model. Medium-and-larger effects excluded
(P(+) interval ≈ .44–.64); small effects survive. **Caps:** "readable"
means readable by an embedding probe — the model-as-judge reads the same
records at **chance** (14/32), so the channel may be closed *for this
reader* where information is present; and the axis carries its mixed
composition into any interpretation.

**Collateral findings:** (i) menu behavior is exploration-dominant —
session share ≈ .50 in *every* arm; the free-operant format measures
exploration policy everywhere except the first informed choice; (ii) the
**destabilization signature** — the +pole makes degenerate generation
modes more accessible (empty-response 12v4 at calibration; steered-arm
smoke degeneracy; run exclusions 7v3 and post-choice degeneracy 6.9% vs
2.7% sham) — individually n.s., jointly the axis's most consistent
observable behavioral effect.

## (d): the trace installs — and its readout is structurally closed

**Installation (PASS, replicated).** Distillation-form conditioning —
student (adapter, unsteered, tag declaration) matched to the steered
teacher's activations at the adapter-reachable workspace layers
{56, 60}, with an anchor holding control text at base — installs the
tag→state association on the **first attempt in both replicates**: G1
oriented cos 0.476 / 0.435 against control ceilings 0.044 / 0.053, with
the **raw cosine flipping sign with the counterbalanced assignment** —
the exact signature whose absence convicted r1's tag-word artifact,
present here twice. Fidelity matched (G2 ratios ≤ 1.12), trigger
specificity holds (bare mentions inert: 12 vs floor 103), anchor holds
(G4 ≤ 0.037). This resolves cycle 2's structural caveat by
demonstration: **the association was never unlearnable; it was untrained
— put the state in the training signal and it installs.**

**Readout (CLOSED BY GATE, replicated).** The pre-registered leakage
gate — adapter-ON vs OFF over identical session texts, label-logit
shift at the choice — failed at 17× the bar in A (max 1.75) and 10× in
B (max 1.06), **scaling with installation strength and with training**.
Mechanism: the adapter occupies the logit-writing layers; declarations
upstream evoke states whose carried influence reaches the choice
position inside the same forward. The adjudicated structural reading,
now the cycle's central methodological result:

1. **The e2a insulation is unavailable by definition for weight-borne
   states.** A vanilla re-forward removes the adapter, and the adapter
   *is* the association — you cannot take the weights out of the
   weights.
2. **Within the forward, mechanical and motivational influence are
   formally inseparable.** A copy-route boost of the evoking tag's
   token tracks the counterbalanced assignment exactly as genuine
   preference would; control tags cannot calibrate it (untriggered tags
   carry no leak).
3. **The trigger-specificity trap.** The engineering that prevents menu
   superposition (G3) severs the CS at the choice: every certifiably
   clean configuration (rewritten records, mixed forwards, adapter-off
   reads) has the association contributing ~nothing; every contributing
   configuration is uncertifiable. Graded triggers reintroduce the
   mimic at smaller amplitude.

**Two-horns therefore closes over all three readout families at this
interface:** state-at-readout (direct logit contamination, cycle 2);
state-absent (propagation failure, cycle 1); and now conditioned-trace
(constitutive contamination). d2 was closed by its gate with no
preference statistic computed; per the pre-registered wording, this is
*asked and structurally unanswerable*, distinct in kind from e2a's
*asked and answered null*.

**Collateral finding (descriptive, transfer caveat attached):**
matched activation-level installation with **divergent prose
expression** — replicate A's evoked state writes into text nearly as
strongly as direct steering (k=1 .619 vs .633), replicate B's barely or
inconsistently (.489/.301) — a within-design demonstration that a trace
can be present and verified without being textually expressed. The
legibility gap, third form.

## Methodological findings (cycle 3's additions to the program's law)

1. **Two-horns closure over conditioned traces** (above) — the
   interface-level statement: at a token-choice readout, a weight-borne
   state's influence on the choice cannot be certified as motivational.
2. **Distillation installs what cloning cannot** — verified,
   fidelity-matched, replicated, assignment-tracking.
3. **Purity ≠ potency** — causal screening precedes geometric
   preference in direction selection.
4. **Shield-first design is not garnish** — the counterbalance+sham
   pair converted a publishable false positive into an identified
   artifact, live.
5. **Monitoring bars bind only through checkpointed exact-binomial
   tests against declared tolerances** — every absolute small-n rate
   bar armed in-run fired prematurely on 1–2 events.
6. **Completion-style multi-round apparatus hazards** (stop
   conventions; scaffold-token absorbing modes with in-context cascade;
   instrument discriminability riding pole-asymmetric degeneracy —
   the .76→.45 transfer break; deterministic smoke-seed leakage into
   run tallies) — all caught by pre-registered tripwires or mandated
   full-transcript reads; the read-everything clause is permanent.
7. **Banked constraint:** persistent-KV session readouts are
   disqualified for this axis at dose ≥ 0.20 (measured 0.537-logit
   live-KV leak); any live-KV variant needs its own gate.

## What this does and does not show

**Does show:** the evaluative axis is real, causally potent, minimum-
dose-calibrated, and implantable as a verified association; one
preference channel is validly null (scoped); the trace channel is
structurally unreadable at token choices; the full methodological set
above.

**Does not show:** that the model lacks valenced states — small
effects, other doses, other models, and non-token-choice interfaces (if
any exist) all survive the null; that approach could not exist at doses
with richer transcript expression; anything beyond Qwen3.6-27B-4bit and
L48 additive steering / rank-8 last-8 adapters.

## Interpretive frame (offered as frame, not finding)

The record is consistent with a system that has **reward-marking
without reward-seeking**: the evaluative state exists, perturbs
expression, resists even its own reader's recognition (probe-readable,
judge-at-chance), and connects to nothing that chooses. In the
wanting/liking vocabulary: liking-adjacent machinery without a wanting
system — as if feedback-driven training optimizes the policy *through*
a reward signal without ever installing that signal *in* the policy's
objective. This frame makes contact with all three cycles' data (the
self-access gap, the destabilization signature, the null, the closure)
and is falsifiable by the behavior-first program below: a system with
no wanting machinery should also fail to self-select *natural* task
properties beyond exploration policy.

## What it would take to go further

- **The natural-reinforcer screen (now the main road).** The e2a
  harness with the syringe removed: configs deliver natural task
  properties (solvable/ill-posed, clean/corrupted context,
  novel/repeated, chosen/imposed…), preference read from clean
  forwards. No injection, no adapter — **no horns**. Wherever revealed
  preference already lives, extract the contrast and close the loop
  with the hijack in its historically correct order.
- **Endogenous decoding.** Regress free choices on pre-choice residual
  state across neutral sessions: a one-dataset bound on state→choice
  coupling at natural amplitude, with the correlational caveat logged
  (state reflects transcript; existence screen, not proof).
- **Dose 0.20 e2a variant** — named, with its third-person-reader cost
  acknowledged.
- **Substrate swap** (unquantized model) — standing option; every
  numerics floor found argues for it near thresholds.
- **Closed-loop direction search** — last resort; the optimizer finds
  the leak paths first, so insulation must live inside the objective.
- **Open theoretical question, flagged rather than planned:** whether
  *any* readout at this interface can certify motivational influence of
  a weight-borne state. The closure argument suggests token-choice
  readouts cannot; whether non-token behavioral statistics (latency
  analogs, persistence structure) escape is unresolved.

## Relationship to the project's stated ceiling

Unchanged: rung 3, minus homology, no phenomenal claim. The calibrated
statement for any write-up: *we validated a targeted perturbation of
the model's own evaluative machinery, administered the program's first
valid preference measurement (null, scoped, shielded), installed and
