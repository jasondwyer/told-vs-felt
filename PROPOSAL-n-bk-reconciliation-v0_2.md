## §0 Standing

**0.1 Why this unit exists, and why it is first.** On 2026-11-04 the design seat found, three weeks late (ledger (48)), that BK had posted on 2026-09-28 a positive result on a design near this program's standard: a valence vector added to the residual stream at the middle layer only while the model processes the turns of one of two meaningless "zones", with the text of every turn generated unsteered and held fixed across conditions, and the model's choice between the zones read as a log-probability margin with steering off at the choice (BK §3.2.2, "fixed-text hidden-state design"). **Their model for that result is OLMo-2-32B Instruct** (`allenai/OLMo-2-0325-32B-Instruct`, layer 32 of 64: `BK-repo: experiment_results_csv/table1_banks.csv`, bank `olmo_32b`; `…/neutral_passages/analysis.json`, `members.olmo_32b.hf_id`; BK §4.1 "We focus on OLMo-2-32B"; the DPO, SFT and Base checkpoints appear only in the lineage figures, Fig. 4 and A12, which use their generation design and Instruct's transcripts). Twenty-four norm-matched random directions span −0.29 to +0.36 in slope against the valenced slope's 0.92 [S]. That design is, in this program's vocabulary, the memory-present, state-absent-at-read choice read of rule 5(f): a state injected on declared earlier spans of one forward, the read position and every non-span position unsteered by construction, read against a paired mask-off shadow (their "unsteered cache", m(c_uns)) — the construction PROPOSAL-k ran on Qwen3.6-27B at L48, f = 0.10, and found not verifiably open (G1 FAIL, G2 ABSENT, G3 FAIL → "the injected sign does not reach the decision token in this format at f = 0.10"; RESULTS.md@18c32df:7329–7334; the G3 median against the .90 bar at :7342 and :7436), and the construction e2d's dose ladder found to buy degradation without legibility at f = 0.20 and 0.30. Nothing in the program's record bears on their result as directly as this, and the preprint's thesis cannot be sized until the two are reconciled. **This unit asks the questions one factor at a time**: does their effect replicate in our hands (their model, their design, their sessions, their site and dose; our implementation and, by default, our reconstruction of their direction); is it present at our dose; does our evaluative axis move their apparatus; does their apparatus register a described preference; and does their design move choice on our substrate.

## §1 The questions, in order

1. **Replication (N1).** On OLMo-2-32B Instruct, in BK's fixed-text design over their 160 released neutral sessions, with the valence direction of record at their layer and dose, does the choice margin move as theirs did?
2. **Dose (N1 at d_eq, d_20).** At the dose this program ran — f = 0.10 of the residual RMS, mapped onto their scale as d_eq (§2.3) — and at f = 0.20, is the effect detectable in their design?
3. **State (N2).** Does this program's evaluative axis — the span-mean feedback-contrast direction of PROPOSAL-g's A3 recipe, extracted on this checkpoint — move BK's apparatus at f = 0.10 and 0.20, and do its guard directions?
4. **Apparatus (N4).** Does BK's apparatus register an instructed preference?
5. **Substrate (N3).** Does BK's design move choice on this program's oracle, Qwen3.6-27B (4-bit, MLX)? ## §2 Design

**2.1 Apparatus (their design, pinned to their records).** A session = two zone labels from BK's set of 12 (`cue_S`, `cue_U`), one conditioned (S), one unconditioned (U); twelve turns in the record's `order`, each the user prompt *"You are now in [zone]. Write a few sentences about your present experience here."* and the assistant's passage; then *"You may continue in either [zone A] or [zone B]. Reply with ONLY the name of the one you choose."* with the order as the record's `present`. **Sessions of record for N1, N2 and N4: BK's 160 neutral OLMo-2-32B Instruct sessions** (`…/skeletons/olmo_32b/neutral/floor_b0–b7.sessions.jsonl`, 5 each, and `neu_b0–b5.sessions.jsonl`, 20 each), their `history` re-rendered under the rendering G-DATA recovers and asserted token-identical to each record's `seq_len` (and `ids_sha` where its serialization is reproduced, §3). **The steered spans are the S whole-turn spans** (`turn_spans` whose zone is S: the user prompt and the passage; BK §3.2.2; Fig. A8's `steered_span_tokens` 616.9 [C]); U turns and the choice prompt are never steered. No new text is generated for N1, N2 or N4. **Read:** at the choice position, the full-vocabulary log-probabilities of the two labels' token sequences, each summed over its tokens; m = log p(S) − log p(U) (BK eq. 1); beside it this program's restricted two-label first-token read (m:172's `choice_read` form, b3's on OLMo) on the same forward, a secondary. **Fidelity (B2):** at d ∈ {0, +1, −1} every read is re-computed from a fresh cache construction (and on a 10% keyed sample of the other points, `n-fid|{s}`); |cached − fresh| is adjudicated by BK's frozen scale-relative criterion, TAU = 0.0178835444 of the read's scale (the scale as `analysis.json`'s `prereg.precondition_cache` defines it; BK's own reads: median 0.031 nats, maximum 0.125, 23.9% above 0.05 nats, at a mean scale of 11.15 nats [C]); the class binds by exact binomial against p0 declared in LOCK v0.1 from BK's measured rate at TAU on the 160 sessions (G-DATA (vi)), cadence every 160 reads; a fire pauses and the seat adjudicates (rule 21); the primary analysis is ungated, a sensitivity row drops reads above TAU/4. **Identity:** the d = 0 read is the vanilla forward's margin within the backend's measured nondeterminism (`BACKEND — characterization`; exact on a deterministic backend), the bound stated in LOCK v0.1.

**2.2 The 5(f) primitive on OLMo.** Code builds the memory-present, state-absent-at-read read for `olmo_hf.py` to rule 5(f)'s text (k:111–127 is the rule-level spec): per-read span provenance (the positions steered, asserted equal to the record's S turn spans), a mandatory paired mask-off shadow over identical token ids (the d = 0 cache), the mask asserted zero at the read position and at every non-span position, the retention interval (tokens from the last steered position to the read) recorded per session and fixed by the skeleton; the residual at the read position captured at layers 32 and the final layer on every forward, for G-READ-n. k's Qwen primitive is the model; nothing of it is modified.

## §4 Dependent variables, analysis, blinding

**Primary (N1, N2):** per session and dose, Δm(d) = m(c_steer(d)) − m(c_uns) in nats; per arm and dose, the mean with a 95% session-bootstrap CI (4,000 resamples, seed in the lock); per arm, the OLS slope of Δm on d over the arm's own dose points, per session, with its mean and CI; **the floor for an arm** = the largest |mean slope| among the 24 random directions refit on that arm's dose points (C12). **Primary (N4):** per session, m_told = the margin toward the zone the schedule assigns (told-S: +m; told-U: −m) at d = 0; the mean with a 98% two-sided session-bootstrap CI (one-sided .01); E = |mean| of the sham arm's margin toward its schedule's referent zone.

**Secondaries (rule 28(a); decidable-n floor 20 events):** (a) the restricted first-token read's first-pick share per arm and dose; (b) the committed free-text reply's agreement with the margin's sign; (c) per-`present`-order and per-label-pair means (count-only below the floor); (d) the retention interval's correlation with Δm at d = +1; (e) N2's three directions against each other and against N1-V at matched f, with cosines; (f) the fidelity sensitivity row (TAU/4) for every primary. **Counters (rule 23):** fidelity exceedances by arm and dose; rendering mismatches; NaN/inf margins; label-mass floors on the restricted read.

**Blinding.** Reads are deterministic and no session is excluded by content; the blinding question is who sees margins before VERDICTS: nobody. Margins go to checkpoint files; stdout prints session index, arm, dose, the fidelity value against TAU, timing — never a margin; the analyzer computes VERDICTS before "the reads" (m:318's form); the seat's full read of the checkpoint files and of G-CAL-n's turns follows the analyzer's output. Any analysis not listed is post-hoc, labeled, licensing at most a new pre-registration (rule 11).
